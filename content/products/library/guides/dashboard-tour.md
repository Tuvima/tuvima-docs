# Find your way around

Take five minutes to find your media and tools in Tuvima Library. Start on Home, choose a [media lane](../reference/glossary.md#media-lane), or search for a title.

## Start on Home

Home shows a banner at the top, a **Pick up where you left off** row, and shelves such as Recently Added. Select a card for details. Use the banner's action to start or resume it.

The banner puts what you are in the middle of first, most recently touched first, then newly added items. It shows up to five slides. A show you have never played appears as the series with **Start watching**; once you play an episode it appears as that episode with **Resume**. To change what the banner may show (turn it off, show fewer slides, leave out books or music, or show only things you have started), open **Settings, Preferences, Home**. A plugin may suggest an item for the banner; its slide says "Suggested by" and the plugin's name, and you can switch each plugin's slides off on the same page.

**Pick up where you left off** is one row of cards, one for each thing you started: the cover, the type, a progress line such as "42% watched" and a round play button. Nothing started means the row is not shown.

The **Watch**, **Read**, **Listen** and **View** chips on Recently Added are shared with this row: choose **Listen** and both rows narrow to music and audiobooks. Library remembers the choice for your profile. They are shared because Home is a mixed page, and you usually want one mood at a time (something to watch tonight, not everything you own).

Recently Added offers All, Watch, Read, Listen, and View as switches you can combine; a type you have nothing of has no switch. It shows when media joined your library. A title may also appear on another Home shelf.

Each shelf shows up to 20 titles. Choose **View all** (the last tile in the row, or the link beside the shelf title) to see the whole list as a grid. Sort it by Recently added, Title A-Z, Year or Recently played, and narrow it with the filters that apply, such as media type, genre, format or progress. More titles load as you scroll. Your sort is remembered for each lane on every device. On a phone, sort and filters are under **Filters**.

## Return to For Me

[For Me](for-me.md) gathers your Continue, My List, Favorites, Collections, Playlists, and Galleries. Empty shelves are left out. The items belong to your active profile.

My List saves items for later; the bookmark in the top bar (the My List tab on a phone) opens it, and Home shows your newest saves under Continue. Favorites, the songs you marked with a heart, is a playlist inside Listen (see Song lists below). Continue follows your progress. Each choice is separate.

## Browse Read, Watch, and Listen

**Read** has books and comics. **Watch** has movies, TV shows, and movie series. **Listen** has music, audiobooks, and playlists.

1. Open a lane and browse its Discover shelves.
2. Choose a scope, such as **Books**, **TV Shows**, or **Music**, for the full browser.
3. Use search and the genre, person, year, and status filters where offered.
4. Choose a sort order or Cards/List view where offered. Every tile is the same size, so there is no size control.
5. Open a card for details, then start the item.

Music starts in Songs view. Click a song to play it. Its menu can open the parent album. A TV show lists the episodes you own, not a provider's full episode catalog.

### Song lists

Songs, Albums, an album's tracks and a playlist all use the same song table, so what you learn in one works in the others.

- Click a row to play it. The heart saves the song to Favorites. Hover a row to add it to the queue, or open its **...** menu for Play next, Go to album and more.
- Choose **Columns** at the end of the header to hide, show or resize columns, or to reset them. Library remembers your choice for each list, on your profile, so it follows you to your other devices.
- To resize a column with a mouse, drag the edge of its heading; the column follows the pointer. Double-click the edge to put that column back to its default width. With the keyboard, focus the edge and use the arrow keys. A column never gets narrower than its minimum, and the width is saved when you let go. Widths are kept separately for Songs, Albums, an album's page and a playlist.
- Click a column heading to sort. A hand-made playlist keeps your own order instead, so its headings do not sort.
- In a playlist you can edit, drag a song by its handle, or select it and press Alt+Up or Alt+Down. With touch or a screen reader, use **Move up** and **Move down** in the song's **...** menu.
- On a phone every song list is a plain list: cover, title, artist and **...**. Tap a song to play it; the heart is in the **...** menu. The Songs page has a search box, Play, Shuffle and a **Sort and filter** button, and an A to Z strip while you sort by title or artist. A phone has no columns, so there is nothing to resize.

### Pick several songs

Tick songs and a bar appears with **Play**, **Add to queue**, **Add to playlist** and, if your role allows editing, **Edit**. **Add to playlist** lists your hand-made playlists and **New playlist**; a playlist that fills itself from rules cannot be chosen, because its songs come from the rules. Songs already in the playlist are skipped, and the notice on the page says how many were added.

Songs go into playlists, not collections, so there is no Add to collection for a song. Ticked albums offer **Add to playlist** (all their songs) and still offer **Add to collection**. Ticked audiobooks offer **Add to collection** only.

On a phone, open a song's **...** sheet and choose **Select**. Tap more songs to tick them. A bar at the bottom offers **Play**, **Add to queue** and **Add to playlist**, and **Done** ends selecting.

On a desktop you can also drag. Drag a ticked song to carry every ticked song (drag an unticked song to carry just that one) and drop them on a hand-made playlist in the Listen rail, on a playlist tile on the Playlists page, or on the **Playlists** heading to start a new playlist from them. The playlist lights up while you hover. Playlists that fill themselves from rules, and Favorites, do not accept drops. Dragging needs a mouse, so touch and keyboard use **Add to playlist**.

### Favorites

Choose **Favorites** in the Listen rail. It is a playlist of the songs you hearted, newest first, in the same song table, with the same play actions. Any column heading can sort it. To put it in your own order, drag a song up or down on a desktop, press Alt+Up or Alt+Down, or use **Move up** and **Move down** in the song's **...** menu (on a phone, in the song's sheet). A song you heart later goes to the top, and the order you make is saved on your profile, so your other devices show it too. **Remove from Favorites** takes the heart off. My List is a separate place for everything else you saved.

## Explore Collections

Collections offers **Discovery**, **Automatic**, **Curated**, **Shelves**, and **People**. Automatic groups connect related works. Curated holds groups someone has put together. Shelves lists eligible book, movie, and audiobook series. People lists primary contributors.

Your own collections also appear in For Me. A library collection enters My List only when you save it and have access. [Learn how groups work](../explanation/how-universes-work.md).

## Search the library

1. Select global **Search** or press **Ctrl+K**.
2. Enter a title, person, series, collection, or playlist.
3. Select a result, or choose **View all results** for the full Search page.

The search overlay opens above your current page. On the full page, narrow results by media, result type, year, and sort order.

## Open details and fix mistakes

Details shows the item's title, art, facts, and actions. Open **Edit** there to fix it, then return to the same page. [Correct an item](editing-items.md) explains which fields you can change.

## Open View

[View](view-personal-space.md) holds personal photos, videos, and other local media in a profile's Personal Space. Its menu has Photos, Galleries, People, and Places. Sharing follows your rights and the chosen scope.

## Check work and settings

The header activity icon opens **Operations** for system work you can access. Operations shows active work and recent batches. Needs Review holds items that need a human decision.

Settings holds folders, providers, profiles, playback choices, and admin tools. Your role controls which tools you see. [Manage library settings](library-settings.md) explains the admin tasks.
