# Apple apps and cars

**None of the apps on this page can be installed today.** This page says what is planned so you know what to expect. Dates are not promised, and the plans can change.

## Apple apps (planned)

An iPhone and iPad app and an Apple TV app are planned, and they come last. Apple apps can only be built and tested on a Mac, which is why they wait.

- **iPhone and iPad.** One app for both is planned to match the Android phone app's screens, including **Edit** opening the Dashboard in the browser. It will be a one-time $4.99 with no further payments, like the Android phone app. Source code for a first version exists in the project, with Home, Libraries, Search, Downloads and Settings, but it has not been through the same checks as the Android app and is not published.
- **Apple TV.** The first version in the project has not been brought up to the shared player yet. It is planned to match the other TV apps, with the same five playback buttons, tool row and side panels, driven by the Siri Remote. TV apps are free.

## Cars (planned)

Both car apps are for listening only: no pictures to browse and no video.

- **Android Auto.** The [Android phone app's](android-phone-app.md) media service now offers the car list (Continue, Listen, Audiobooks and Playlists) and the Now playing card, but it has not been tested in a car or on the Desktop Head Unit yet, and the app is not published.
- **CarPlay.** The code for a first CarPlay audio screen exists, but Apple must first grant the project a CarPlay audio permission before a signed build can be given to anyone. It is planned to arrive with the Apple apps.

When they arrive, both car apps will show the same short list: **Continue**, your **Listen** shelves, **Audiobooks**, **Playlists** and **Recently played**. The Now playing card shows the title, a second line and the cover. Music has Previous, Play and Next, plus Shuffle and Repeat. Audiobooks have back 10 seconds, forward 30 seconds, Previous and Next chapter and speed choices. Search is by voice through the car's assistant. A Vault item never appears on the Now playing card.
