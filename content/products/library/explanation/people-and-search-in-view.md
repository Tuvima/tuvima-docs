# People and search in View

Photos search understands people, places and years, and **People** finds and groups the faces in your own photos. All of it runs on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## One search bar

Click the Photos search bar and suggestions open under it, grouped as **People**, **Years**, **Places**, **Cameras** and **Tags**, each with a count. Choose one and it becomes a chip; chips combine and narrow the timeline, and the chip's X takes it off. Plain text still searches dates, devices, places and tags. On a phone the suggestions open as a bottom sheet.

Suggestions only ever come from photos you are allowed to see in the library you are viewing.

## Words inside photos

**Read words in my photos** (under **More filters** on Photos) is off until you switch it on. Once it is on, Library reads the words in your own photos, such as screenshots and receipts, in the background, and a search for a word finds them. Photos in the Vault, in the Shared Library or in other people's spaces are never read. Turn it off and nothing more is scanned.

## People

Library finds faces in the photos in your own space, turns each into a short list of numbers, and groups look-alikes. It never looks in the Vault or at anyone else's photos.

- **Who's this?** shows the biggest groups that have no name yet. Name one, and Library offers other groups that may be the same person with **Is this Sam?**: untick any face that is someone else, then choose **Yes**, **Not Sam** or **Skip**. Nothing is named without a yes.
- A person's page has one menu: **Rename**, **Merge with…**, **Choose cover photo**, **Hide this person** and **Remove from all photos…** (asked once).
- In the photo viewer, the **People** section shows each face in the photo, with the name or **Name this face**.
- Names already in your files' metadata appear as named people and are never overwritten.
- A count on **People** in the View menu shows how many groups are waiting for a name.

While faces are still being found, People fills in as groups form and shows how far Library has got.

## The face tools

Finding and telling faces apart uses two small models (YuNet and SFace, both listed on the [attributions page](../policies/attributions.md)). Library downloads them once, the first time they are needed, and lists them under **Settings > Tools**. They are never part of the app itself.

## Your control

- **Recognize people in my photos** is on by default and can be switched off at the bottom of the People page. Off means nothing is scanned and People says so.
- **Delete all face data** removes every face position, group and name Library made. Your photos are not changed.
- Face data is stored in Library's own data store, is included in backups like your other personal settings, and is deleted when the profile is deleted.

## Places

A line under the Places map names where the map pictures come from, and **Built-in map only** keeps the map from asking any online service for pictures.
