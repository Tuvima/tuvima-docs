Tuvima wants helpful people to be recognised for being helpful, without turning conversations into a contest. Reputation is how a community says "thank you, that was useful".

## Votes and reactions

Every post, on the front page and in communities, has two separate controls:

- **Votes** say "more like this" or "less like this". Choose the up or down arrow, and choose it again to take it back. Votes decide what rises, and both counts are shown.
- **Reactions** say how a post made you feel: **Love**, **Funny**, **Insightful**, **Thanks**, **Wow**, **Sad** and **Angry**. Choose **React** to see them all. You can give one reaction per post and change or take it back at any time. A reaction never pushes a post down, so it is fine to say a post made you angry or sad.

You can vote and react on the same post. Others see how many of each a post has, never who gave them. The author of a post can see who said **Thanks**, so a thank-you reaches the person it was meant for.

## Reputation: earned from other people

**Reputation** is a public score with achievements, earned only when other people value what you wrote:

- up votes and reactions to your posts and replies. Insightful and Thanks count most, Love and Funny a little; Wow, Sad and Angry share a feeling and earn nothing,
- replies from different members,
- people quoting you,
- your answer being accepted.

A few rules keep it fair:

- **Many people beat one fan.** Each extra reaction from the same person counts for less, and one person can only give you so much in a day.
- **Steady help beats one lucky post.** After a post earns a lot, it earns more slowly.
- **Good conversations count.** If your reply starts a thread of replies that people value, you earn a share of it. A pile-on does not.
- **Links alone earn nothing.** What you write with a link earns like any post. A link that starts a real discussion can earn a "Sparked a conversation" achievement.
- **Disagreeing is free.** A down vote never takes Reputation away. Points are taken back only if the post that earned them is removed for breaking the rules, or the votes behind them turn out to be fake (see [keeping votes honest](#keeping-votes-honest)).
- **Nobody can edit it.** Not a community owner, not Tuvima staff. Reputation is your history and does not fade.

## It stays in each community

Your Reputation belongs to one persona in one community. It never travels to another community or another persona. That way, points earned in one place cannot be carried somewhere else, and nobody can connect a private persona to you by comparing scores.

Every community uses the same reactions and the same rules, so a score means the same thing everywhere. On the front page, Reputation is kept per category, so you can see that someone knows their stuff in Science without them being an expert on everything. Each category page will show who has been **Helpful this month**.

## Recognition, not power

Reputation never pushes your own posts up and never gives you moderation powers. A post rises only on votes and how recent it is.

## Levels

A member's card in a community shows their **Role**, their **Level** and their Reputation. The one [achievement](achievements.md#show-one-by-your-name) a member chooses to show also appears beside their name. Your level shows next to your name on every post, and on the front page your Reputation in that post's category shows too.

- **Role** is given by the owner, for example Moderator.
- **Level** is a ladder. Every community starts with five ready-made **Journey** levels, which the owner can rename or replace:

| Level | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Wanderer** | New here, with a few [newcomer limits](newcomers.md) for the first days. |
| **Companion** | A full member of the community. |
| **Pathfinder** | Takes part often and is valued by others. |
| **Warden** | Long-standing and trusted. Needs keeping up. |
| **Elder** | Honoured by hand for long-standing people. |

- You climb by earning Reputation, taking part on many different days and being valued by different people. A community can also ask for a number of posts and replies, or days since joining.
- Some levels are **given by hand only**, like Elder. Nobody climbs into them on their own.
- On your own persona page you can see what the next level needs and how far along you are. Only you see this.
- Only one thing lowers a level on its own: if a level needs keeping up, a long time away (about six months) moves you down to the nearest level below that doesn't, and never below the second level. Falling below a number never moves you down.

The front page uses the same five names. A persona is a Wanderer for its first days, and Elder on the front page is given only by Tuvima staff, with a reason, and the person is told.

### Safety steps

However a community names its levels, every level sits on one of four **safety steps**: **New**, **Member**, **Regular** and **Trusted**. Members never see them; owners choose them on the levels page, with the first two levels always on New and Member. The step, never the level's name or number, decides how much a vote counts and what a member can do on the front page. So a community with 50 levels can't give anyone more say than a community with five.

Each step also has a Tuvima minimum that owners can't lower. To be on the **Regular** step a member must have taken part on at least 10 days with nothing removed in the last 30 days. For **Trusted**, at least 30 days with nothing removed in the last 90. Until they meet it, they stay on the step below, whatever their level.

## What owners can change

Each community has one ladder. On the community's **Member levels** settings page, the owner (and admins) can:

- have up to **50 levels**: add, remove, rename, describe and reorder them,
- set what each level needs: Reputation, active days, the number of different people who valued your posts, posts and replies, and days since joining. Raising a number never moves anyone down,
- choose each level's safety step, whether it needs keeping up, and whether it is given by hand only,
- turn the **Sparked a conversation** and **Top contributor** achievements on or off. All other [achievements](../owners/achievements.md) are managed on their own page,
- hide Reputation numbers in the community, so only levels show.

Removing a level moves its members to the nearest level below; the page says where they go before you confirm. A level that opens a [category or forum](../owners/area-access.md) can't be removed until that area's setting changes.

The owner can also set a member's level by hand. It stays where it is until the owner chooses **Return to automatic**, which puts the member back on the level they have earned. The member gets a notice with their new level, and the change is kept in the community's moderation record. A newcomer has to graduate before their level can be moved.

## Achievements

Achievements are marks of recognition, separate from Reputation. They never add to your Reputation or your level, and they never open areas or give powers. Two of the ready-made ones are tied to how others value your words:

- **Sparked a conversation**: a post that got replies from at least five different members whose replies people valued. You can earn it many times.
- **Top contributor**: among the ten people who earned the most Reputation over the last 30 days, in a front-page category or in a community. You hold it while you stay there; when you drop out it stays in your list marked as past.

Communities and the front page have many more, such as years as a member and accepted answers. They count fairly, using the same filtered numbers as Reputation, so votes from vote rings and removed posts never count. [Achievements](achievements.md) explains how to earn them, how rare they are, and how to show one by your name.

## When a post is removed

On the front page, members settle most concerns themselves through [member moderation](member-moderation.md). When members hide a post, the points it earned are taken back while it is hidden, and come back if another look shows it again. It counts as a removal for the [safety steps](#safety-steps), but the author's level does not drop.

Tuvima staff step in on the front page only for illegal content and legal notices. Every staff removal is public: anyone who opens the post sees **Removed by Tuvima staff**, the rule it broke, the reason staff wrote and the date. The points the post earned are taken back and its author moves down one front-page level (never below the first) for 90 days. If staff later restore the post, its points and the author's earlier level come back, and the author is told.

Tuvima staff never step into a community's own moderation. When a community's moderators remove a post, its points are taken back, and levels stay in the owner's hands.

## What money can and cannot do

Some communities offer [member tiers](../owners/member-tiers.md), such as **Supporter**, and paid tiers will come later. An owner may let a tier skip the newcomer stage. A tier never raises your level beyond that, and never changes your Reputation, your votes or how far your posts reach.

## How votes are weighed

Up and down votes from members on a higher [safety step](#safety-steps) count for more, from a quarter of a vote for someone brand new up to three votes at the top. How much a single vote counts varies at random each time, within the member's limit, so nobody can plan a "power vote". It evens out over many votes. Reactions never change a post's place.

## Keeping votes honest

Reputation and Trending only mean something if the votes behind them are real. Tuvima does three things quietly in the background.

### A hidden trust check

Tuvima gives less weight to accounts that act like bots, that Tuvima staff have caught breaking the front-page rules, or that vote together in coordinated groups.

- It can only hold someone back. It never boosts anyone.
- Nobody sees it, not the member, not other members and not Tuvima staff. It is not a score and is never shown anywhere.
- It wears off over time, so an account that behaves normally gets back to full weight.

Most members are never affected by it.

### Vote rings

A **vote ring** is a group of accounts that mostly vote together, or mostly vote for each other. A check runs every night to find them. While a group is flagged, its members' votes on the same post count as one vote.

Tuvima staff can review a flagged group. If they confirm it, the group's recent votes are taken back, along with the Reputation those votes earned. Anyone who earned points honestly from other people keeps them.

### Sudden floods of votes

When a front-page post suddenly gets a flood of votes, they count gradually rather than all at once. A real crowd still counts in full within a few hours. A coordinated push loses the moment it was trying to win.

## Folding and hiding on the front page

On the front page, members settle concerns about posts together. Randomly picked members who are not involved take a second look and can add a label, fold a post behind **Show anyway**, or hide it, but only when most of them agree. Without clear agreement, the post is left alone. Members who help fold posts that reviewers then find are fine lose the ability to fold for a while, and can lose a level if it happens again.

[How member moderation works](member-moderation.md) explains who reviews, how many must agree and what the author can do.

Inside communities, moderation stays with each community's own moderators for now. See [reporting and moderation](reporting-and-moderation.md).
