In each community you use one persona: your **global persona** (`handle @ tuvimaid.com`, the same name everywhere) or a **private persona** made for that community. You can change your mind later. Switching affects only the community you are in. Your other communities are not touched.

To start, open the community, choose **Joined** to open **Your membership**, then choose the switch row. It says **Switch to my global persona** or **Switch to a private persona**. If the row is missing, the owner has not allowed that switch in this community.

## Switch from private to global

Use this when you want people to know you across Tuvima. It is the same persona, so nothing is lost.

1. Choose **Switch to my global persona**. If you have no global persona yet, the row says **Set up your global persona first** and links to your Tuvima profile.
2. The next page shows **How it looks to others**: your global name on your posts, with "formerly" and your old private handle beside it.
3. Choose **Switch to** your global handle. If the owner wants a moderator to approve switches, the button says **Ask to switch** instead. You can add a message and the page warns that moderators will see your global persona when they decide. See [if the owner asks for approval](#if-the-owner-asks-for-approval).

What stays: your posts, level, achievements and roles. What others see: your posts now show your global persona, and members can see that your old handle became your global one.

This cannot be reversed. Going back to private means deleting your persona here (see below).

## Switch from global to private

Use this when you want a fresh start with a private name. It is the same as leaving and starting again, and it is a clean break.

1. Choose **Switch to a private persona**.
2. The page says **This means leaving as** your global handle. Your persona here is deleted, and your posts follow the community's rule for deleted personas (see [delete your persona](join-a-community.md#delete-your-persona-in-a-community)).
3. Type a **New private name** and choose **Delete and start private**.

You start as a newcomer: your level and achievements do not move across. There is no 30-day undo for this one, because it is a fresh start by design.

Some communities use **Same wait as a new persona**. Then you delete your persona here and can come back with a private name only after the community's wait, 30 or 90 days. The page shows the date. Only you see it.

The owner of a community can switch to global but cannot switch to private, and only current members can switch.

## What other people see

- **"formerly @old"** appears next to your name on your persona page in that community, for as long as the owner chose. It shows only in that community. Your global persona never lists names from any community, so nobody can tell where you belong from your profile.
- **Name changed recently** is a small label on your posts for 30 days, if the owner has it on.
- **Earlier names here** on your persona page in that community lists your earlier names while the owner's time lasts. When the time ends, the old name is deleted.
- When you go from global to private, where the owner shows name history, members can see your old global name for a while. The screen tells you this before you confirm.
- Moderators see an earlier handle in the moderation record as "as @old", so a record about you does not get lost in a switch. Warnings, notes and record entries follow you.

Owners set these times. See [switching and name history](../owners/leaving-and-coming-back.md#switching-personas).

## The 30-day limit

You can switch once in each community every 30 days. Tuvima ID keeps count privately. If you try too soon, the page says when you can switch again, and only you see that date. See [your personas](/products/id/guides/your-personas.md#switching-personas-in-a-community).

## When you can't switch

The page explains why and shows no button:

- You are suspended or banned in that community.
- A report about you is open there.
- Your persona there is being deleted.
- You are the owner and want to switch to private.
- The owner does not allow that switch in this community.
- You have no global persona yet.
- You switched within the last 30 days.

## If the owner asks for approval

When the owner requires approval for a switch to global, the request waits in the [approval queue](../owners/warnings-and-filters.md#the-approval-queue). The page shows **Waiting for a moderator** and the date you asked. Choose **Withdraw** to cancel. Until a moderator decides, you stay as you are.
