Good conversations don't only happen on the front page. When a conversation in a public community gets people talking, Tuvima can show it to people beyond that community, on **Home**, **Trending** and **New**. Opening it takes you to the conversation itself, in its community.

## What you see

A community conversation shows as a card that says where it comes from, for example **From Synth Tinkerers · 412 members · started by Modwheel**. The name is the one the person uses in that community.

- **Open** the card to read the conversation in its community.
- **Reply** opens it with the reply box ready. If you haven't joined, you can usually [reply as a visitor](visitor-replies.md). If the community doesn't take visitor replies, or the conversation is members only, the button says **Join to reply**.

You never see more than 2 cards in a row from the same community.

## Which conversations can appear

A conversation can appear outside its community only when all of these are true:

- the community is public and listed, and its owner hasn't turned off **Show popular threads on the Tuvima portal**,
- the forum is open to everyone, has no content warning, and hasn't been switched off with **Show on the Tuvima portal**,
- enough different people have taken part. A conversation needs reactions or replies from at least 3 different people, not counting the person who started it. Bigger communities need more: 2% of their members, up to 25 people,
- it hasn't been removed or held by moderators, and the person who started it isn't held back by Tuvima's checks for fake activity.

Private communities, unlisted communities and members-only forums never appear. Paying gives no place here.

## Topics

Every conversation has a topic, such as Music or Cooking. It starts as the topic of its forum. When you start a conversation you can pick a different one, and Tuvima suggests one from the words you used. Owners can lock a forum's topic.

If a conversation looks like it's in the wrong topic, choose **Wrong topic** in the menu on its first post. The community's moderators see it and can change the topic. It doesn't count against anyone.

Health, religion, sexuality and politics are sensitive topics. Conversations about them only reach your Home if you chose that topic yourself, by following it or tapping **More like this**. They don't appear in the general Trending and New lists; you find them on their own category page.

## Found useful

Home keeps a little room for things outside your usual interests. Part of that room goes to conversations that trusted members of a community marked **Insightful** or **Thanks**. This is measured against the community's size, so a helpful answer in a small community can reach you too. These show the reason **Found useful in** and the community's name.

## Popular with people like you

Home can also lift a topic or community that's popular with people whose interests are like yours. This only happens when at least 10 different people are behind it, and Home never says who they are. The reason reads **Popular with people who like** and the topic. You can turn this off on your Your feed page in Tuvima ID, with **Learn from people with tastes like mine**. Turning it off also stops your interests counting for anyone else, within an hour.

## For apps

Apps can read Trending and New, including community conversations, through the public feed: titles, links and counts only. Topics, Wrong topic and the owner switches are not available to apps.

## Plain English summary

Popular conversations from public communities can now reach Home, Trending and New. Each one is marked with its community and opens there. Only open, listed places can appear, and only once enough different people have taken part. Owners and forums can opt out. Sensitive topics only reach people who chose them.
