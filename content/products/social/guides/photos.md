Photos are on their way. Everything on this page is built and switches on once Tuvima's photo safety checks are set up. Until then, posts can link to pictures hosted elsewhere.

Photos are free in every community. Nobody pays to post them or to see them.

## Add photos

1. While writing a conversation, a reply or an edit, choose **Add photos**.
2. Pick one or more photos. Each one uploads while you keep writing.
3. Under each photo, write a short description of what is in it. People who use a screen reader hear it, and it shows under the photo when it is opened full screen.
4. Use the arrows to change the order, or **Remove** to take a photo out.
5. Post as usual.

A post with photos doesn't need any words, and a conversation with photos doesn't need a title: Tuvima shows the first line of your words, or "Photo", instead. Some forums still ask for a title.

Up to 10 photos fit in one post. Your community may allow fewer.

## What happens to your photo

- **Hidden details stay on your device.** Before a photo leaves your phone or computer, Tuvima makes a smaller copy and leaves behind the hidden details cameras add, such as where the photo was taken. Tuvima's servers clean each photo again when it arrives.
- **Every photo is checked.** Photos known to be illegal are blocked and reported to the authorities. Photos that may be adult or violent are handled the way your community chose (see below).
- **Sizes for every screen.** Tuvima keeps a few sizes of each photo, so phones load a small one and large screens a sharp one.
- **Members-only stays members-only.** A photo in a private community, or in an area only some members can open, can only be opened by people who can read the post. Its addresses stop working after a few minutes, so a copied link doesn't let anyone else in.

Tuvima doesn't use end-to-end encryption. Tuvima staff can see photos when they need to, for example to deal with a report.

## How many photos

To keep spam away, newcomers start slowly:

- While you're new to a community (your first 3 days there, or your first 5 posts), you can add 1 photo to each post and 3 a day.
- After that, your community decides how many photos each level can add.
- Every account can add up to 20 photos a day across Tuvima in its first week, and 200 a day after that.
- An account that joins more than 10 communities in one day stays at the newcomer numbers for a week.

If you reach a limit, Tuvima says so and when you can add more.

## Viewing photos

- Tap a photo to open it full screen. Swipe, or use the arrow keys, to move between photos.
- A photo that may be adult or violent can be shown blurred, behind **Show photo**. Nothing is shown until you ask.
- If a moderator removes a photo, the rest of the post stays and a short note says a photo was removed.

## For owners and admins

Open **Settings › Photos** in your community to choose:

- **Whether members can add photos at all.**
- **What happens to photos that may be adult or violent:** cover them behind **Show photo** (recommended), hold them until a moderator looks, or show them as they are. Tuvima's own rules still apply either way.
- **The newcomer numbers.** You can make them stricter than Tuvima's, never looser.
- **Photos for each level:** whether a level can add photos, how many to a post and how many a day.

In **Categories and forums**, each forum can switch photos off, or ask for a title on every conversation even when it has photos.

Your owner, admins and moderators always use the highest numbers in your community.

## For moderators

- **Remove one photo** from a post from the full-screen view. The rest of the post stays, and the removal goes in your moderation record.
- **Held photos** (in Moderation) lists photos waiting for a moderator when your community chose to hold them. Choose **Show** to let readers see one, or **Remove**.
