The front page is Tuvima's shared space: a feed of discussions and links from everyone, sorted by category. It is where broad topics, like news or technology, live, so that communities can stay focused.

## Browse the feed

At the top of the feed, choose how to sort it:

- **Trending** (the default) shows what people are taking part in right now. It counts votes and how many different people replied, and gently lets older posts fade. Votes from profiles less than a week old count for less, so a burst of new accounts cannot push a post to the top. It covers the last 14 days.
- **New** shows the newest posts first.

Next to the sort are **categories**. Pick one to see only that subject. Each category has subtopics, for example Gaming has PC, Console, Mobile, Tabletop and Esports. A category page also shows its popular tags and some communities about that subject.

A **tag** is a short label, like `board-games`, that a poster adds. Choose a tag to see everything with it.

## Vote

Every post has an up and a down vote. You need to be signed in, and you cannot vote on your own posts. Others see the totals, never who voted.

## Post to Tuvima

1. Choose **Post to Tuvima**. You post with your Tuvima profile.
2. Under **What are you posting?**, choose **A link** or **A discussion**.
3. For a link, paste it and choose **Check link**. The title fills in for you, and you can change it.
4. Pick one to three **categories**, and a subtopic if you like. Each category gets its own conversation.
5. Add up to five **tags** and, if the post needs it, a **Content warning**.
6. Choose **Post**.

If a link is already being discussed, Tuvima tells you where, so the conversation stays in one place. Once that conversation is more than 14 days old you can **Start a new round anyway**.

:::note
New profiles can post links to the front page after they have made 3 posts that others can see. This keeps out accounts that only exist to spread links.
:::

## Search

**Search** finds front page posts, communities and people's Tuvima profiles. To search inside one community, use that community's own search.
