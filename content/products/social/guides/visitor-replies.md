You can reply in most public communities without joining them first. You reply as a **visitor**, under your global persona, and a small **Visitor** label is shown beside your name.

## Reply as a visitor

1. Open a conversation in a public community you haven't joined.
2. Choose **Reply**. The box says **Replying as @yourname** with the **Visitor** label, so you can see exactly who will be shown.
3. The first time you reply in a community, tick **I agree to the rules**. The community's rules are one tap away. You are only asked once in each community, and again if the owner publishes an important change to the rules.
4. Write your reply and choose **Reply**.

You'll get an alert in your bell when someone answers you, the same as for any reply.

## What visitors can and can't do

Visitors can **reply** in public forums. To do anything else, join the community:

- start a conversation
- react or vote
- add photos or links
- use chat channels

To keep communities calm, a visitor can reply up to **5 times a day** in each community. Join to reply more often.

Visitors don't earn a community level, community reputation or achievements there. Those start from the day you join, and earlier visitor replies aren't counted towards them.

Useful visitor replies do build your reputation in the conversation's topic on the Tuvima portal, for example in Gardening. That topic reputation is never shown as reputation inside the community you visited.

## When you can't reply as a visitor

The reply area tells you why, in plain words:

- **Only members can reply here.** The owner has turned visitor replies off, a moderator has made this conversation members only, or the forum is for members. Choose **Join to reply**.
- **You left this community.** You still have your persona there. Rejoin with it to reply.
- **You can take part again after a date.** You recently deleted a persona in this community. Only you see the date.
- **You can't reply in this community.** No reason is given, so that nothing about your other personas is shown.

Private communities and members-only spaces never take visitor replies.

## Joining later

If you enjoy a community, choose **Join**. You pick a persona then:

- **Your global persona.** Your visitor replies stay yours and keep the Visitor label. Your level and achievements start from the day you join.
- **A new private persona.** This is a clean cut. Your earlier replies stay under your global persona and nothing moves. Nobody, moderators included, is told that the two are connected.

Tuvima can't stop people guessing, though. If your new persona carries on the same conversation straight after your visitor reply, people may put two and two together. Leave a little time, or pick a different conversation.

Once you have a persona in a community, you always reply as that persona there. You can't reply as a visitor and as a member in the same community.

## Privacy

Before you reply, the community asks Tuvima ID one question: may this person reply here as a visitor? Tuvima ID answers only **yes** or **no**. It never says why, and it never tells the community which of your other personas, if any, belongs there. The community can only ask while you are replying, and only about that one community: when you press Reply, Tuvima ID gives your browser a short pass that works there and nowhere else, for a few minutes. Reading a community never shows who you are. Your persona is only shown when you choose to reply.

Moderators can remove a visitor's reply, warn them, or stop them replying in the community. Stopping a visitor works like a ban, and Tuvima ID applies it to every persona of that account in that community, so it can't be dodged by joining under another name.
