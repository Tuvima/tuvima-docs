Tuvima gives you a simple, reasonable expectation of privacy while you take part in conversations. It is not built to hide everything from everyone, including from Tuvima itself, and we want you to know the difference.

## What others can see

- **Your persona**: handle, display name and picture, for the persona you use in that place.
- **What you post**: the front page and every public community can be read by anyone, including people who are not signed in and search engines. Unlisted communities are left out of search, but anyone with the link can read a public one. A **private community**, and a members-only area inside a community, can be read only by the members allowed in. A private persona hides who you are, not what you post.
- **Your activity, to moderators**: a community's moderators and owner see when you joined, how many posts you have made and the last day you visited.
- **Your join answers, to reviewers only**: if you ask to join a community, the people who review requests see your answers. They are deleted 90 days after a decision.
- **Your reports, to moderators only**: when you report a post in a community, that community's moderators see which persona reported it. The person you reported is never told who reported them.
- **Your concerns, to nobody**: when you raise a concern about a front-page post, the members who review it, the author and other readers never see who raised it. Reviewers' answers stay private until the case closes, and the post's public moderation history shows counts, never names. A concern about illegal content goes to Tuvima staff, who see which persona sent it.

## Switching and renaming

You can switch between a private and a global persona in a community, and a private persona can ask the admins for a new name. Here is what each shows:

- **Going from private to global** shows your global persona in that community, with "formerly" and your old private handle beside it for as long as the owner chose. Moderators see your global persona before they approve, if the owner asks for approval. You can't go back to private without deleting your persona there.
- **Going from global to private** deletes your persona there and you start as a newcomer. Where the owner shows name history, members can see your old global name for a while; the switch page tells you before you confirm. **Deleting and coming back after the wait** is the route with no link at all: nothing connects the two personas.
- **A rename** shows "formerly @old" in that community only, for the owner's time. Your global persona never lists names or communities.
- **A name hidden as offensive** is not sent to other members at all. They see **Name hidden**. Only you and staff who can rename or suspend see the real name.

Tuvima ID keeps a private log of switches. See [your personas](/products/id/guides/your-personas.md#switching-personas-in-a-community) and [switching](switch-persona.md).

## What they never see

- **Your email address.** It stays in Tuvima ID. Tuvima itself never receives it, and nor does any community.
- **Your other personas.** If you use a private persona in a community, no member, moderator or owner can tell it is you.
- **Trackers.** There are no tracking scripts or third-party analytics on Tuvima, and we do not sell your data. Tuvima keeps its own records, such as where you got to in a conversation, to run the site for you.

## Links and embedded players

When you post a link, Tuvima's own servers fetch a small preview card for it, so readers' browsers do not contact the other site just by reading. Players for video and other media load only when a reader presses **Play**. From that moment, the other site's cookies and terms apply.

## Search engines and AI

Search engines may list public pages so that people can find communities. We ask known AI-training crawlers not to collect Tuvima pages.

## The privacy you do not have

Tuvima is a place for conversations, not a secret messaging service:

- The Tuvima team can read what is stored on Tuvima, including posts in private communities and members-only areas. Posts are not end-to-end encrypted, the kind of protection where even the company running a service cannot read your messages. Private keeps posts from people who are not members; it does not make them secret.
- Tuvima ID knows which personas are yours, and the people who run Tuvima can reach that link when there is a real reason, such as abuse, safety or a lawful request.
- Moderation actions are recorded and kept.
- Tuvima keeps a hidden trust check on each account, to give less weight to accounts that act like bots, break front-page rules or vote in coordinated groups. It can only hold someone back, never boost them, it wears off over time, and nobody sees it, Tuvima staff included. [Reputation and levels](reputation.md#keeping-votes-honest) explains it.
- Deleting a post removes it from view. Full, permanent deletion is still being designed.

Nothing we build can stop you sharing something you later regret. Use the same caution and good judgment you would in any public place. [Tuvima ID's privacy page](/products/id/explanation/privacy.md) explains the details, with a diagram of what is kept where.
