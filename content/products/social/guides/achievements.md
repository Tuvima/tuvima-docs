An **achievement** is a small mark of recognition for something you did, such as your first reply, a year as a member, or having your answer accepted ten times. Each one has an **emblem**: a shape, a symbol and two colours.

Achievements are recognition only. They never open an area, add to your votes or give you any powers. [Levels](reputation.md#levels) and [member tiers](../owners/member-tiers.md) do that. There is no score either: every achievement stands on its own, and nothing adds them up into a total.

Every community has its own achievements, set up by its owner, and the Tuvima front page has its own too. Yours in one place never mix with another place.

## What you can see, and who else can

| Who sees it | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | The achievements you have earned, and how rare each one is. |
| **Only you** | Your progress toward achievements you have not earned yet, such as "74 of 100". |

On someone else's page you only ever see the achievements they have earned.

## Earning one

When you earn an achievement, a short note slides up on screen for a few seconds. It names the achievement, says how rare it is and has a **View** button. Hover over it, or tab to it, and it stays open. If you prefer less movement, it appears without sliding. An entry also appears in your [bell](keep-up.md#the-bell).

Achievements never send an email or a phone alert.

Two kinds arrive quietly, in the bell only and without the note on screen:

- achievements you already qualified for when a rule was added or made easier. Tuvima looks back at past activity once, at a quiet time, and gives you what you had already earned.
- year achievements, such as **2-year member**.

## Achievements in a series

Some achievements come in **steps**. For example, **Helpful** has steps at 10, 25, 50, 100 and 250 accepted answers. Each step is its own achievement, shown together as a series. A step reads **Step 3 of 5**. On the close-up card (see below) the series shows every step, ticked once earned.

## Secret achievements

An owner can keep an achievement secret. Until you earn it you see a "?" and the name **Secret achievement**, with no description. Its rarity and the number of people who hold it still show. Your progress toward it stays hidden too. Once you earn it, everything appears.

## How rare is it?

Each achievement shows how many members hold it, for example **Held by 6% of members**. Tuvima works this out each night, so a new achievement may show no figure for a day. Below 5% an achievement is called **Rare**, and its emblem shines. An owner can make an emblem always shine or never shine.

## The Achievements tab

In each community, **Achievements** is one of the tabs in the community's top band. It shows your own collection in three lists, each with a count:

- **Earned**: what you hold, with the date. An achievement a moderator gave you by hand says **Given by hand**, and any public note from the giver appears under it.
- **Working on**: achievements you have started, with a progress bar and a figure such as "74 of 100". Only you see these.
- **Still to earn**: the rest that are open to you, so you can see what is possible.

Above each list you can search, and choose **Where** (the whole community, or one forum, category or tag), **Kind** and **Order**. Earned lists newest first by default, Working on lists the closest to finished first, and Still to earn lists the rarest first. You can also order by name.

On a computer, a side column adds **Recently earned in the community**: the five newest achievements earned there. It only shows what you are allowed to see, and leaves out secret ones you have not earned.

To see another member's collection, open their name and choose **All achievements** on their page. You will see their **Earned** list only.

## The close-up card

Choose any achievement to open its card. It shows:

- a large emblem, its name, and how it is earned in plain words,
- its kind, where it counts and, in a series, which step it is,
- the date you earned it, or **Retired** if it is retired,
- how rare it is,
- **Your progress**, if you have not earned it yet. Only you see this.
- the other steps of its series,
- **Who holds this**, a list of members, newest first, 50 at a time. It leaves out anyone you are not allowed to see.

If you hold the achievement, the card also has two buttons: **Shown by my name** and **Add to showcase**. Owners and admins see a **Manage** link to the rule behind it.

## Show one by your name

Next to your name on your posts in a community you can show one small emblem. **Nothing is shown until you pick one.**

On your Achievements tab, find **Showing by your name**, choose **Change**, and pick any achievement you hold there, or **Show none**. You can also choose **Shown by my name** on an achievement's card. Choosing it again turns it off. You pick one for each community and, separately, one for the front page.

## Your showcase of six

Your page in a community opens with an **Achievements** section, above your pinned posts. It shows up to six achievements, how many you have earned there, and an **All achievements** button.

Until you arrange it, your page shows the six rarest ones you hold. To choose your own, open **Your showcase** on your Achievements tab, choose **Arrange your six**, and use **Up**, **Down** and **Remove**. You can also choose **Add to showcase** on any card. When you have six, remove one first.

## Community goals and limited-time achievements

Two kinds of achievement appear as cards on the community's home page while they run.

- **Community goals** are shared targets. The card shows the community's total so far against the target, for example "620 of 1,000". Under it, **You helped** shows your own part. Only you see that figure. When the target is met, everyone who helped earns the achievement.
- **Limited-time achievements** can only be earned between two dates. The card shows how many days are left, when it ends and your progress. It says **You earned it** once you have.

When a goal or a limited-time achievement is over, its card leaves the home page.

## Kept for good

An achievement you earn is yours to keep. There are two exceptions:

- If the posts or votes behind it are removed for breaking the rules, or the votes turn out to be fake, the achievement can be taken back. This is the same fair counting [Reputation](reputation.md#keeping-votes-honest) uses.
- An achievement that is given by hand can be taken back by whoever can give it.

**Standing achievements**, such as **Top contributor**, are held only while you are in the top places. When you drop out, it stays in your list marked **past**, for example "Earned 12 Sep 2026 · past".

## Retired achievements

An owner can retire an achievement. Nobody new can earn it, but everyone who already holds it keeps it, marked **Retired**.

## Year achievements

Year achievements count from the day your persona joined that community. On the Tuvima front page they count from when the persona was created. Each persona counts separately, so a different persona starts from zero.

## Achievements on the Tuvima front page

The front page has its own achievements, such as **Explorer** (conversations you start there) and **Lorekeeper** (Insightful reactions from different members). They show on your persona's page on tuvima.com, with the same showcase, tabs and close-up cards. Everything there counts from when your persona was created.

### Achievements given by hand

A few front page achievements are given by Tuvima staff. Every one is listed on the public page **Hand-given by Tuvima staff**, which anyone can read, signed in or not. Each entry shows the achievement, the member, the date and the reason staff gave. It names the giver only as "Tuvima staff". Achievements taken back are listed there too.
