Most communities can be read without joining, by anyone. Join to post, reply and get notices. A **private** community can be read only by its members; see [joining a private community](join-private-community.md).

## Find a community

Choose **Explore** in the menu, or **Find a community** on Home if you haven't joined one yet. Explore suggests communities for you and shows what is growing; see [Explore and your communities and follows](explore.md). For the full directory, choose **See more**:

- **Search communities** by name or subject.
- Filter by **category**.
- Sort by **Most active**, **Newest** or **Largest**.

**New and rising** shows young communities, under 90 days old, where people are taking part. Two of its places are picked by chance, and the selection changes through the day, so newer communities get a fair chance to be found.

Some communities are **unlisted**: their owner has chosen to leave them out of the directory and search. You can still open one if someone gives you its link.

## Join

1. Open the community and read its **rules**. Each community writes its own, on top of [the rules everyone follows](community-rules.md).
2. Choose **Join**. If the community asks people to be approved first, the button says **Ask to join** instead; see [joining a private community](join-private-community.md).
3. Choose how to appear:
   - **Join as** your global persona, `handle @ tuvimaid.com`.
   - **Create a private persona for this community**, with its own handle, name and picture. Members and moderators cannot see that it is connected to your global persona.

Think about the choice. Your global persona lets people recognise you across Tuvima. A private persona suits a community where you would rather keep that part of your life separate, such as a health support group. Remember that it hides who you are, not what you post: in a public community anyone can still read it.

Some communities ask new members to take part for a while before everything is switched on. See [being new to a community](newcomers.md).

## Mute instead of leaving

If you only want a break, choose **Joined** at the top of the community to open **Your membership**, then **Mute this community**. You get no alerts from it, and with your global persona it also stays off your Home. You stay a member. Choose **Unmute this community** to turn things back on.

With a private persona, muting stops the alerts only.

## Leave

Choose **Joined**, then **Leave this community**. The next page shows what stays and what ends, then choose **Leave**.

What stays:

- your persona, so the name you used here is kept for you,
- your posts, shown under your name as usual,
- your level and achievements.

What ends:

- your roles here, such as moderator,
- alerts from this community,
- its place in your community list and on Home.

A [member tier](../owners/member-tiers.md) that is still paid for stays attached, and you still get notices about it.

An owner cannot leave their own community yet. Handing over ownership is planned.

## Come back

Open the community again and choose **Rejoin as** your persona. You always come back as the persona you had there, with your posts, level and achievements where you left them. To use a different name, ask for a rename after you rejoin.

Each community's owner decides how people come back:

- **Any time**: you rejoin straight away, even if the community normally asks people to be approved or invited.
- **A moderator approves**: you agree to the rules and choose **Ask to come back**, with an optional message for the moderators. The page shows "Your request to come back is waiting" until someone decides. You can choose **Withdraw request** while you wait. If it is not approved, the page says when you can ask again.

If the community asks people to be approved or invited, choose **Been a member before? Come back** under the join button.

Someone who is banned or suspended in a community cannot come back while that lasts.

## If a moderator removed you

A moderator can remove someone from a community without banning them, for example for spam or for being off-topic. You get a notice with the reason. Your persona stays, and so do your posts unless they were removed as spam.

To come back, open the community and choose **Join**. Depending on the community, you rejoin straight away or choose **Ask to come back** and wait for a moderator. You come back as the same persona.
