Every community can be read without joining, by anyone. Join to post, reply and get notices.

## Find a community

Choose **Find a community** on the front page.

- **Search communities** by name or subject.
- Filter by **category**.
- Sort by **Most active**, **Newest** or **Largest**.

**New and rising** shows young communities, under 90 days old, where people are taking part. Two of its places are picked by chance, and the selection changes through the day, so newer communities get a fair chance to be found.

Some communities are **unlisted**: their owner has chosen to leave them out of the directory and search. You can still open one if someone gives you its link.

## Join

1. Open the community and read its **rules**. Each community writes its own, on top of [the rules everyone follows](community-rules.md).
2. Choose **Join**.
3. Choose how to appear:
   - **Join as** your global persona, `handle @ tuvimaid.com`.
   - **Create a private persona for this community**, with its own handle, name and picture. Members and moderators cannot see that it is connected to your global persona.

Think about the choice. Your global persona lets people recognise you across Tuvima. A private persona suits a community where you would rather keep that part of your life separate, such as a health support group. Remember that it hides who you are, not what you post: anyone can still read the community.

Some communities ask new members to take part for a while before everything is switched on. See [being new to a community](newcomers.md).

## Leave

Open the community's menu and choose **Leave community**, then confirm. Your posts stay. You can still find them, and delete them, from **Your posts here** or from your [Tuvima ID account page](/products/id/guides/your-account.md).

If you rejoin with the same persona later, your progress as a member is kept.

An owner cannot leave their own community yet. Handing over ownership is planned.
