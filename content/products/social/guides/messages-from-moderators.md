When a community's moderators write to you, the message comes **from the community**, not from one moderator. You see the community's name and a **Community** label, never which moderator wrote it. When you reply, your reply goes to the community's whole moderator team, so any of them can answer and nobody has to wait for one person.

These conversations sit in your [messages](messages.md) with everything else, under the community's name and icon.

## Write to the moderators

1. Open the community and choose **Message the moderators** from its menu.
2. Pick what it is about: **A question**, **About a decision** or **Something else**.
3. Write your message and send it.

You can start up to 3 of these a day in each community. If you already have an open conversation with that community's moderators, you are taken back to it instead of starting a new one.

## Reply to a warning or a removed post

When the moderators give you a warning, remove or hide one of your posts, decline a name change or remove you from the community, the notice has a **Reply to the moderators** button. Your reply opens a conversation that says what it is about, for example "About your post Cheap patch cables". If you already replied to that notice, the button takes you back to the same conversation.

If you have left a community or been removed from it, you can still reply to the moderators for 30 days. After that the conversation closes.

## Who can read it

The top of the conversation says who can read it: **everyone on that community's moderator team**. Nobody else in the community can, and your other private messages stay private.

- The moderators see your persona, as they would anywhere in the community. They never see your Tuvima ID or your email address.
- The moderator team can keep private notes about the conversation. You never see them.
- These conversations are **not** end-to-end encrypted, the same as your other messages. See [how private is a private message](messages.md#how-private-is-a-private-message).

## Closed conversations

When the moderators are done, they close the conversation and you see **The moderators closed this conversation**. Write again to reopen it with the team.

## What you can do in the conversation

- **Mute alerts** if you do not want to be told about new messages.
- **Report a threat to Tuvima** if a message threatens violence or puts someone in danger. The report goes to Tuvima staff, not to the community's moderators.

Messages with the moderators reach you whatever your [message settings](../../id/guides/who-can-message-you.md) say, because they come from a community you are in. Block does not apply to a community, so it is not offered here. Reactions, replies to a single message and editing are not available in these conversations, to keep them clear and on the record.
