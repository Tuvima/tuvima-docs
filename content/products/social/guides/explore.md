**Explore** is where you find new communities, tags and people. **Your communities and follows** is the one list of everything you have joined or followed, for all your personas.

## Explore

Choose **Explore** in the menu (on a phone, in the bar at the bottom of the screen).

- **Search** at the top finds communities, tags, people and conversations. Pick what to search, or leave it on **Everything**.
- **Categories** narrow the communities to one subject.
- **Communities for you** suggests communities, each with a short line saying why, such as "Because you're in Retro Consoles" or "Popular in Gaming". If you are not signed in, Explore shows communities to visit instead.
- **Tags people are using today** shows the tags on conversations started on Tuvima in the last day.
- **Growing this month** shows the communities that most people joined in the last 30 days.

### How these lists are ordered

Suggestions come from the communities you are in, the categories you follow and recent activity. **Nobody can pay to be shown in Explore.**

- **Unlisted** communities never appear.
- **Private** communities, and communities you can join only by invite, are never suggested.
- Communities you are already in are left out.
- A sensitive category, such as health, is used only if you follow it yourself. Being in a community about it never leads to suggestions.

On a computer, Home also shows a few of these suggestions and tags beside your feed.

## Search results

Search results are grouped into **Communities**, **Tags**, **People** and **Conversations**. Choose **See all** on a group to see more of that kind.

**People** lists global personas only. A private persona can be found only inside its own community.

## Your communities and follows

Choose **Communities and follows** in the menu. The list has four tabs:

- **Communities**: every community you are in, with your role, your alert setting, how many new conversations it has and when you last visited.
- **People**: the people your global persona follows.
- **Topics**: the Tuvima categories you follow.
- **Conversations**: conversations you set to **Every reply**.

Search the list by name. On the Communities tab you can show only communities with something new, muted ones or quiet ones, and sort by recent visits or by name.

### All your personas in one place

If you use private personas, the list shows the communities for all of them together, with which persona you use in each. Choose a persona at the top to see just that one.

Your browser builds this combined view by asking for each persona's list separately. Tuvima never receives a request that links your personas together, so nobody, including community moderators, can learn from it that they belong to one person.

### Change many at once

Tick the rows you want, or **Select all** to choose everything that matches your search. Then:

- **Change alerts**: Normal, New conversations or Muted. See [keep up with conversations](keep-up.md) for what each means.
- **Mute** the communities.
- **Pin** up to 12 communities to the top of your side menu. Pinning works for communities you are in with your global persona.
- **Leave** the communities, after you confirm. You cannot leave a community you own.
- **Unfollow** people or topics.
- **Back to usual** for conversations, so they no longer alert you about every reply.

Each row's own **⋯** menu does the same for one row.

### Tidy up

A community you have not opened for 6 months, and have not muted, counts as **quiet**. When you have quiet communities, the page offers to show them, so you can mute or leave the ones you no longer need.
