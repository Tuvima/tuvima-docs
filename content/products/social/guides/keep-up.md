Tuvima helps you come back to the conversations you care about, without flooding you.

## The bell

The bell in the menu collects notices addressed to you. Filter by **All**, **Replies**, **Mentions**, **Quotes** and **Your posts**. Each profile has its own bell, so a private profile's notices stay in that community.

A post tells you one thing only: a reply beats a quote, a quote beats a mention. Several replies in a row are bundled together.

## Choose what you are told about

Open the notification settings, **What you're told about**:

- Under **Tell me about**, switch **Replies to my posts**, **Mentions**, **Quotes** and **New posts in conversations I watch** on or off.
- Under **Watch automatically**, choose **Conversations I start or reply to**, **Only conversations I start** or **Never; I choose each one**.

You are always told when moderators hide, remove or restore one of your posts, because you should always know.

For email, see [choose which emails you get](/products/id/guides/email.md) on Tuvima ID.

## Watch and save

- **Watch** a conversation to hear about new posts in it.
- **Save** a post to come back to it later.

## Your You page

**You** brings it together:

- **Communities**: the communities you are in, with how many new conversations each has.
- **Saved**: posts you saved.
- **Watching**: conversations you watch.

## Edit your profile

Choose **Edit your profile** from **You**:

- **Display name**: up to 50 characters, does not need to be unique.
- **About you**: up to 500 characters.
- **Picture**: a still photo or picture file (JPEG, PNG or WebP) up to 2 MB.
- **Handle**: you can change it once every 30 days. Your old handle stays reserved for you for a while, your profile shows "formerly" with the old handle, and mentions of the old handle still reach you.

Your profile page shows your handle, name, picture, about text and earlier names. It never lists the communities you are in.
