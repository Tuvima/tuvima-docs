Tuvima helps you come back to the conversations you care about, without flooding you.

## The bell

The bell in the menu collects everything addressed to you, from every persona you use, in one list. Each notice shows which persona it is for; a private persona shows only its community's name. Filter by **All**, **Mentions** and **Moderation**.

A post tells you one thing only: a reply beats a quote, a quote beats a mention. Several replies in a row are bundled together while you have not read them.

## Choose what you are told about

Every conversation, forum, category and community has an **Alerts** button (a bell). It offers three levels.

In a conversation:

- **Every reply**: your bell shows each new reply, bundled while unread.
- **Normal**: only replies to you and @mentions. This is where everything starts.
- **Muted**: nothing from here, not even mentions.

In a forum, category or community:

- **New conversations**: your bell tells you when anyone starts one.
- **Normal**: replies to you and @mentions.
- **Muted**: only moderator notices.

The nearest choice wins. A conversation's own choice beats its forum's, a forum's beats its category's, and a category's beats the community's. Choosing **New conversations** for a forum does not mean every reply in it; pick **Every reply** in a conversation for that.

Starting or replying to a conversation no longer watches it for you. You stay on **Normal** until you choose otherwise.

You are always told when moderators hide, remove or restore one of your posts, or change your role, because you should always know.

Each community has an **Alerts** page that shows your usual level there, any forums you set differently, and the conversations that email you.

## Email about a conversation

In a conversation's **Alerts**, switch on **Also email me**. Email is off until you do, and it only covers that one conversation.

- You get **one email** saying there are new replies. After that, nothing more about that conversation until you open it again.
- Emails from all your Tuvima apps are gathered into **at most one email every 8 hours**, and none arrive during your quiet hours.
- Opening the conversation, or reading its notice in the bell, starts the cycle again.

### Why we limit email

Twenty replies should never mean twenty emails. Fewer emails mean less clutter in your inbox, less of your activity sitting in other mail systems, and lower running costs that keep Tuvima affordable. The bell always has everything; email is only a nudge to come back.

To stop, switch **Also email me** off, use the stop link in any email, or turn off a community or pause all email in [Tuvima ID](/products/id/guides/email.md).

## Phone alerts

Phone alerts show a notice on your device when something new reaches your bell. They work with the installed app. Tuvima ID delivers them, the same way it delivers email, so your quiet hours apply.

:::note
Phone alerts are still being switched on during Early Access. Until then, the setting says they are not available yet.
:::

- There is one switch per device, in the notification settings, so you can have alerts on your phone and not on your laptop.
- Alerts follow your bell, for every persona. You only get what you would see in the bell.
- During your quiet hours alerts wait. When they end, each device gets one alert saying how many arrived.
- **Show the post's text in alerts** is a Tuvima ID setting and is off by default. Even when it is on, text is never shown for posts or forums that have a content warning.
- Signing out stops alerts on that device.

See [email and alerts](/products/id/guides/email.md) on Tuvima ID for quiet hours, the text setting and your list of devices.

## Save

**Save** a post to come back to it later.

## Your You page

**You** brings it together:

- **Communities**: the communities you are in, with how many new conversations each has.
- **Saved**: posts you saved.
- **Watching**: conversations you set to **Every reply**.
- **Drafts**: posts you started and have not sent. See [save a draft](posting.md#save-a-draft).

## Edit your profile

Choose **Edit your profile** from **You**:

- **Display name**: up to 50 characters, does not need to be unique.
- **About you**: up to 500 characters.
- **Picture**: a still photo or picture file (JPEG, PNG or WebP) up to 2 MB.
- **Handle**: you can change it once every 30 days. Your old handle stays reserved for you for a while, your persona shows "formerly" with the old handle, and mentions of the old handle still reach you.

Each persona has a public page that shows your handle, name, picture, about text and earlier names. It never lists the communities you are in.
