Each community looks after itself: its moderators deal with reports and keep the conversation on track. Tuvima's own team steps in for serious harm, illegal content and spam across communities.

## Report a post

1. Choose **Report** on the post. You need to be signed in.
2. Pick a reason: **Spam**, **Harassment or hate**, **Breaks this community's rules**, **Illegal or dangerous** or **Something else**.
3. Add a short note if it helps. It is required for **Something else**.
4. Send it.

The community's moderators see your report and which persona sent it. The person you reported, and other readers, do not.

You can follow your reports under **Your reports**, which shows whether each is open or has been reviewed. To protect everyone's privacy, it does not say what action was taken.

To stop misuse, you can report each post once, and up to 10 posts an hour.

## What moderators can do

| Action | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Hide post** | The post is hidden from readers while moderators look into it. It can be shown again. |
| **Remove post** | The post is taken down. It can be restored if it was a mistake. |
| **Suspend** | The member cannot post, reply, react or join for a set time: 1 hour, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 30 days or a custom period. |
| **Ban** | The member cannot take part in that community until the ban is lifted. They can still read public conversations. |

Bans and suspensions apply to the person, not just one persona, so starting a new private persona does not get around them.

Every action is written to the community's moderation record, so decisions can be checked.

## If your post is moderated

You get a notice in your bell, with the reason if the moderator gave one. It never says who acted or who reported you. Readers see **Hidden by moderators** or **Removed by moderators** in its place, unless the community's owner has turned these notes off.

If you think a decision about a community was wrong, a moderator or owner of that community is the first person to ask. For a problem with Tuvima itself, use the [contact form on Tuvima ID](/products/id/guides/your-account.md#contact-the-tuvima-team).

## When Tuvima steps in

Tuvima staff can pause a community while they review it. Reading continues, but posting, editing, voting and joining stop for members, usually for a day or two. The community's moderators can still tidy up. Staff can also suspend a persona across all of Tuvima for breaking [the rules everyone follows](community-rules.md).
