A Custom feed is your own feed, with your own name, made from any mix of topics, tags and public communities. The communities can be ones you've joined or ones you haven't. For example, "Weekend projects" could bring together Gardening and Woodworking, the tag #upcycling, and two communities you like to read.

## Make a feed

1. In the side menu, under your communities, choose **New custom feed**. On a phone, open the menu from **You**.
2. Give the feed a name, up to 40 characters.
3. Search for topics, tags or communities, and choose **Add**. What you've added shows as chips, grouped by kind. Remove one with its ×.
4. Pick what to show first, **Hot** or **New**, and choose **Save feed**.

You can have up to 20 Custom feeds, each with up to 50 topics, tags and communities. To change or delete a feed, open it and choose **Edit**. Deleting a feed asks you to confirm and can't be undone.

## Reading a feed

A feed uses the same posts as Home. A post from a community you haven't joined says **From** and the community's name, with a **Not joined** label. If the community allows it, you can still reply as a visitor.

Open a post's **···** menu and choose **Why am I seeing this?** to see which part of your feed brought it in, for example "In your feed: Gardening". Switch between **Hot** and **New** at the top. That switch doesn't change the default you saved.

## More like this and Less like this

Tapping **More like this** or **Less like this** on a post inside a Custom feed changes only that feed. It doesn't change your Home. Tapping them on Home works the same as before and changes Home.

## What you've muted stays muted

Anything you've muted or snoozed for Home, such as a topic, tag, community or persona, stays hidden in your Custom feeds too. Each feed has a short note saying so, with a link to manage your mutes on your Your feed page in Tuvima ID.

## Sensitive topics

You can add health, religion, sexuality or politics to a feed. Their posts then show in that feed only. Adding them doesn't make them appear on your Home.

## When a community goes away

If a community in your feed becomes private or unlisted, or you're no longer allowed in it, its posts stop showing straight away. You won't get an alert. Next time you edit the feed, it is marked **No longer available**, so you can remove it.

## Privacy

- Only you can see your Custom feeds. They aren't shown on your persona, can't be shared yet, and apps can't read them.
- Your feeds are kept in Tuvima ID, beside the order of your communities, so they can mix communities you joined with different personas. When you open a feed, the community side uses its list only to build that page, and doesn't keep it.
- Your feeds are included when you download what Home knows about you, and they are deleted with your account.
