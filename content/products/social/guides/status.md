Your status tells people in a place whether you are around. Choose it from the menu in your own panel (see [Find your way around](getting-around.md#your-own-panel)).

## The four statuses

- **Here**: you are around.
- **Away**: you are not at your screen.
- **Do not disturb**: you get no pop-ups and no sounds.
- **Hidden**: you are not shown in any who's-here list. You can still read and write, and your messages still show as normal.

There is no "last seen". Tuvima never shows when you were last around, and the community's online count is only a number: it counts people active in the last 15 minutes and names no one.

## One main status

Your Tuvima ID keeps one **main status** for you. Change it in one place and it changes everywhere you use your Tuvima persona, on every device you are signed in on.

## Private personas

A [private persona](switch-persona.md) is kept apart from your global persona so nobody can tell they belong to the same person. For that reason a private persona keeps **its own status**. If it followed your main status, someone could notice that two personas change status at the same moment and guess they are one person.

If you want a private persona to follow your main status, open its settings and tick **Use my main status here too**. This is your choice for that persona only, and you can untick it at any time.

## What others can see

People in a place see only the status of the persona you use there. No place is ever told about your other personas or their statuses. See [Privacy](privacy.md) for how personas stay separate.
