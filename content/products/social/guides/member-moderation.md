The Tuvima front page has no moderators of its own. Instead, members look after it together. When someone raises a concern about a post, a small group of members who are not involved is picked at random to take a **second look**. If enough of them agree, the post gets the lightest fix that works. If they don't, the post is left alone.

:::note[Front page only, for now]
Member moderation works on the Tuvima front page today. Communities will be able to switch the same tools on later. Until then, a community's own moderators handle its reports, as described in [reporting and moderation](reporting-and-moderation.md#report-a-post-in-a-community).
:::

## Raise a concern

On a front-page post, choose **Raise a concern**. You pick one reason from two groups.

**Something to tidy**: the post is fine, but in the wrong shape or place.

- **Off topic**
- **Wrong place**: it belongs in another category.
- **Duplicate**: it is already being discussed.
- **Out of date**
- **Spoiler**

**Something harmful**:

- **Scam, fraud or spam**
- **Threat or private details**, such as someone's home address shared without their permission
- **Harassment or hate** aimed at a person or a group
- **Illegal content**

**Illegal content** is different from the rest. It goes straight to Tuvima staff, and members are never asked to look at it.

Reviewers, the author and other readers never see who raised a concern. When a case you raised a concern about closes, you are told how it ended.

[Reporting and moderation](reporting-and-moderation.md#raise-a-concern-on-the-front-page) has the steps.

## When a case opens

A concern does not change anything on its own. It becomes a **case**, a question for reviewers to answer, when:

- one member on the front page's Regular [safety step](reputation.md#safety-steps) or higher raises it, or
- two different members raise one, whatever their step.

Harmful concerns open a case the same way. More concerns about the same post join the case that is already open.

The post stays readable while it is checked. It is **folded** only when harmful concerns reach the usual fold threshold: enough members on the Regular or Trusted step raise them within one day, with a Trusted member counting in full and a Regular member counting half. A folded post shows **Members raised a concern. Independent members are checking it.** with a **Show anyway** button. It is not removed and keeps its votes.

A post can have one tidy-up case and one harmful case. After that, new concerns about it are kept but do not open another case.

## Who takes the second look

Reviewers are picked at random from members who:

- are on the front page's Regular safety step or higher, and
- have a global persona that is at least 30 days old.

These people are never picked for a case:

- anyone who raised a concern about the post,
- the post's author,
- anyone who has replied in the conversation,
- anyone who usually votes together with the author or with someone who raised a concern.

Nobody is offered more than 5 cases a day.

## Take a second look

If you are picked, you get an alert in your bell, and a **Second look** line with a count appears in the **You** menu. Open the case to see:

- the post, as it was when the concern was raised (you are told if the author has edited it since),
- the concern and the front-page rule it is about, if one was picked,
- the author's side, if they have given it.

You never see who raised the concern, and you see the author only as "A member" with their level. In a harmful case, links in the post are hidden while it is checked.

Then choose the lightest fix that would put it right:

- **For something to tidy**: **Leave it alone**, **Add a label**, **Fold it** or **Not sure**.
- **For something harmful**: **Yes, hide it**, **No, it is fine**, **It is something else** (then pick a lighter fix: a label or a fold) or **Not sure**.

**Not sure** counts as an answer and is never held against you. If you would rather not answer, choose **Skip this case** and someone else is asked.

Your answer is private. Nobody, including the other reviewers and the post's author, sees any answers or how many people have answered until the case closes. When it does, you are told how it ended and whether your answer matched.

## How many must agree

| Case | Reviewers | Needed to agree |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Something to tidy | 5 | 4 on the same fix |
| Something harmful: hide it | 7 | 6 |
| Something harmful: a lighter fix | 7 | 6 on the same fix |
| Another look, asked for by the author | 9 fresh reviewers | 7 to keep it hidden |

Answers are not added together: in a harmful case, 5 people saying "hide it" and 2 saying "fold it" is not clear agreement.

Reviewers have 12 hours. If too few have answered by then, more members are picked, once, for another 6 hours.

**Without clear agreement, the post is left alone.** Any fold lifts, and nothing is passed to Tuvima staff. Tuvima staff can adjust these numbers over time.

## What everyone sees

| Outcome | What readers see |
| --- | --- |
| **Label** | A small tag on the post, such as **Out of date**, and one line: **Members labelled this Out of date.** The post is fully readable. |
| **Fold** | The post is collapsed behind **Folded by members** with the reason, how many reviewers agreed and a **Show anyway** button. |
| **Hidden** | The post's words, title, links and photos are replaced by **Hidden by members**, the reason and the count, for example **Hidden by members: scam, fraud or spam. 6 of 7 reviewers agreed.** |
| **Left alone** | Nothing changes. |

Moving a post to a better category is not available yet.

## Moderation history

Every post has a public **Moderation history**. It shows what was raised, what was decided and when, with the counts, such as **6 of 7 reviewers agreed**. It never shows names: not who raised a concern, not who reviewed, and not the author's reply. Anyone who can read the post can open it from the **History** link on a label, fold or hidden note.

## If a concern is raised about your post

You are told straight away, with the reason and any rule. You can give your side once, in up to 280 characters. Reviewers see it beside your post, but it is never shown in the public history.

If members hide your post, you can **Ask for another look** once, within 7 days. Nine fresh reviewers, none of whom took part before, decide. Seven must agree to keep it hidden; otherwise it is shown again. Their answer is final.

While your post is hidden by members:

- the points it earned are taken back; they come back if another look shows it again,
- it counts as a removal for your record on the [safety steps](reputation.md#safety-steps),
- your level does not drop.

## Keeping it fair

- If you helped fold a post and the reviewers decide it is fine, you cannot help fold anything for 30 days. If that happens again within 90 days, you also move down one front-page level. A case that ends without clear agreement does not count against you.
- You can help fold up to 5 posts a day.
- You can raise a concern about each post once, and up to 10 posts an hour. You can't raise a concern about your own post, or one you are reviewing.

Some quiet checks stop an organised group from using the process against someone:

- **People who often raise concerns together count as one.** If the same people keep raising concerns on the same posts (3 shared posts in 30 days) and most of those concerns are found fine, their concerns count as one person's. They are also never picked to review the same case.
- **Your record matters.** If most of your recent concerns were found fine by reviewers (6 of your last 10), your concerns count for half. Only concerns reviewers found fine count against you. Reports of illegal content, and cases without clear agreement, never do.
- **A cleared author is protected for 30 days.** When reviewers find one of your posts fine, for the next 30 days a concern about any of your posts still gets a second look, but the post stays fully visible while it is checked. Illegal content is the only exception.
- **A sudden pile-on slows down.** If concerns arrive about 3 or more posts by the same person, or in the same thread, within 24 hours, those posts are not folded straight away, and nobody who raised one of those concerns is picked to review them.
- **An edited post can be checked again.** If a post is edited after reviewers found it fine, it can get one more check, judged on the new version.

These checks never show anyone your answers, your record or who raised a concern. Tuvima staff watch how the process is working overall (how often reviewers agree, how many cases go unanswered, and random spot checks of closed cases), but a spot check never overrules what members decided.

## What Tuvima staff do

Tuvima staff no longer judge ordinary cases. They handle:

- reports of illegal content,
- legal notices,
- suspensions across all of Tuvima,
- guarding the process itself, so it stays fair.

A post reported for illegal content may fold while staff decide, and staff can remove it with a public reason.
