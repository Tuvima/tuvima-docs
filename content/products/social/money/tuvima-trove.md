**Tuvima Trove** is an optional membership for you as a person. It adds perks across Tuvima and lets you support the communities and people you care about. Everything that makes Tuvima work stays free.

## The levels

| Level | Monthly | 6 months | Yearly | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bronze** | $2.99 | - | $29 | Link up to 3 of your own self-hosted servers, 100 Silver Castar a month, a Bronze mark, early previews |
| **Silver** | $5.99 | $32 | $59 | Everything in Bronze, 1 Ember a month, 300 Silver Castar a month, avatar and banner styles, bigger photos |
| **Gold** | $9.99 | $54 | $99 | Everything in Silver, 2 Ember a month, 600 Silver Castar a month |
| **Platinum** | $19.99 | $109 | $199 | Everything in Gold, 3 Ember a month, 1,000 Silver Castar a month |

Bronze comes first. The other levels follow soon after.

If you own a community, Trove also lets you claim short community names (Bronze 1, Silver 2, Gold 3, Platinum 5), and Bronze members get a monthly Watchfire spark. See [Short names, Watchfire and Kindling](watchfire-and-kindling.md).

## Longer plans include Gold Castar

Pay for 6 months or a year and you get [Gold Castar](castar.md#gold-castar) straight away, to give as gifts. Monthly plans don't include Gold, but you can always buy it.

| Level | 6 months | Yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silver | 300 Gold | 750 Gold |
| Gold | 600 Gold | 1,500 Gold |
| Platinum | 1,000 Gold | 2,500 Gold |

Gold never expires.

## What Trove never does

- It never ranks your posts higher or shows them to more people.
- It never gives you extra votes, Reputation or moderation powers.
- It doesn't work in 18+ communities. Those use [Onyx Castar](castar.md#onyx-castar) instead.

## Showing your Trove mark

Each persona can show or hide its Trove mark. Nobody can tell from your mark which other personas are yours.

## Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel at any time. Your perks last until the end of the time you paid for.

If you ask for a refund, you get back the unused part of your plan, minus the value of any included Gold you have already given away. Unspent included Gold is removed. Gifts you have already given stay given.

*Example:* Mira pays $199 for Platinum yearly, which includes 2,500 Gold. After 4 months she cancels, having given 1,000 Gold in gifts (worth $10). Her refund is 8 months' worth, $132.67, minus $10: **$122.67**.

## Buying in the app or on the web

You can join Trove on the Tuvima ID website or in the Tuvima app. Phone app stores charge a fee on purchases made inside apps, so prices in the app are a little higher. What you get is the same, and the people you support receive the same either way. Where the store allows it, the app shows you a link to the cheaper price on the web.
