A community earns money in four ways. The owner sets up payouts once, in Tuvima ID, and the money goes to the community's payout account.

## Ways a community earns

| Source | What the community gets |
| --- | --- |
| **Gifts and tips** on posts in the community | A share of every gift and tip, starting at 5% |
| **Ember** from Trove members | A fixed amount for each Ember, paid by Tuvima |
| **Paid tiers** ([member tiers](../owners/member-tiers.md)) that members subscribe to | 80% of the price |
| **Add-ons** the community sells | 80% of the price |

Gifts on a community's posts count for the community wherever they are given: inside the community, from the Tuvima front page, or on a post embedded on your own website.

## The community share grows

The more people support the community, the bigger its share of gifts. Supporters are the different people who gave a gift, a tip or Ember in the community, or subscribed, in the last 90 days. The extra share comes out of Tuvima's part, never the member's.

| Supporters in 90 days | Community share of gifts |
| --- | --- |
| any | 5% |
| 1,000 | 6% |
| 10,000 | 8% |

If the number drops, the community keeps its share for 90 more days.

*Example:* a member's post in your community gets a $10 gift. The member earns $6.50 and the community $0.50. Once you have 10,000 supporters, the community earns $0.80 and the member still earns $6.50.

## Ember support

Turn on **Accept Ember** in your community settings to let Trove members support you each month. You see how many supporters you have and what you can expect to earn; members never see a money amount. Members choose where their Ember goes each month, so your supporter count can change from month to month. You can thank supporters with a supporter mark, a supporters-only chat room or a thank-you message.

18+ communities can't receive Ember. If a community becomes 18+, its Ember ends that day and goes back to the givers, and the community is paid for the days before.

## Getting paid

Before your community can take payments or set up paid tiers, the owner confirms who they are in Tuvima ID. The first payout is available 30 days after that check, then earnings are paid automatically once a month. At first, payouts are open to payees in the United States.

## What money never does

Money never buys a higher place in the Tuvima front page, search or anyone's feed. A community that earns more is not shown more.
