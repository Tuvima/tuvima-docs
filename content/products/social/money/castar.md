**Castar** is Tuvima's currency. It comes in four kinds, and each does one job.

| Kind | How you get it | What it's for | Expires? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Silver** | Comes with [Tuvima Trove](tuvima-trove.md) every month | Awards on posts and items for your persona | Yes, at each renewal |
| **Gold** | Buy it, or get it with a longer Trove plan | Gifts that earn creators real money | Never |
| **Ember** | 1 to 3 a month with Trove Silver, Gold or Platinum | Supporting a community or a person each month | Yes, if you don't place it |
| **Onyx** | Buy it, if you are a verified adult | Support and gifts in 18+ communities only | Never |

Castar never ranks posts higher or shows them to more people.

## Silver Castar

Silver comes with your Trove every month: 100 with Bronze, up to 1,000 with Platinum. Spend it on awards, such as a Glimmer, Compass, Bright Star or Hearth, and on items for your persona. Silver has no cash value, can't be sent to people directly and doesn't work in 18+ communities. Anything you don't spend is gone at your next renewal, and a fresh amount arrives.

## Gold Castar

Gold is for gifts, such as a Lantern or a Phoenix. When you give a gift, the person who made the post earns real money from it. See [Earning on Tuvima](earning.md).

- 100 Gold is worth $1 of gift value. Bigger packs include a little bonus Gold.
- Gold never expires. You can't cash it out yourself; it's for giving.
- Unspent Gold you bought can be refunded within 30 days of buying it.
- Gold doesn't work in 18+ communities.
- Gifts are anonymous unless you choose to show your name.

## Ember

Ember is how Trove members support a community or a person every month, at no extra cost. You never see a money amount; Tuvima pays the receiver for you.

- Silver gives 1 Ember a month, Gold 2 and Platinum 3. You can't buy Ember.
- Give at most one Ember to each community or person a month.
- Ember doesn't renew on its own. Each Ember supports that place until your next renewal, and then you choose again, which can be the same place. Ember you don't place fades at your renewal.
- Supporters get an Ember supporter mark there, and sometimes a thank-you from the owner, such as a supporters' chat room.
- You can't give Ember to yourself, to your own communities, or to 18+ communities.

## Onyx Castar

Onyx is the only currency used in 18+ communities, and nothing else works there. Any adult who has passed the age check can buy it, with or without Trove. It's sold separately, through a payment provider that handles adult content.
