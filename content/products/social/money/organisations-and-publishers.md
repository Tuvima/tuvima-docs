Businesses, newsrooms, clubs and publishers can run their communities on Tuvima like anyone else, for free. Organisation plans add the tools a team needs: seats for staff, your own rules for what staff earn, reports and more control.

Organisation plans are billed separately from anyone's personal Trove. You find them on the Trove page under **For organisations**.

## Routing gifts and tips to staff

When readers send gifts or tips to a staff member's work persona, your organisation decides what happens to that money. You can:

- pass everything to the staff member,
- keep everything for the organisation, or
- split it, for example 50% to the person and 50% to the organisation.

You can set one rule for everyone, or different rules by section or by person. The money arrives in your organisation's payout account, with a statement of who earned what, so you can pay staff through your own payroll if you prefer.

Staff always know where they stand. Each staff member has an **eligibility page** that shows the rule that applies to them, what they have received and what they will be paid. Readers see the same gift buttons either way.

*Example:* a newsroom on Trove Business sets the rule "Reporters keep 70% of tips; the newsroom keeps 30%." A reader tips a reporter $20 for a story. The reporter's part of the tip is $17 (the community's $1 share also goes to the newsroom). The reporter's eligibility page shows $11.90 for them, and the newsroom's statement shows $5.10 kept. The newsroom pays it with the reporter's next salary.

Personal personas are never affected. If the same reporter posts in a hobby community under their own persona, what they earn there is theirs.

## How publishers earn

A publisher can earn in five ways:

1. **Gifts on articles.** Readers give gifts on your articles wherever they are shared and discussed on Tuvima, even in other communities and when someone else posted the link. The person who shared it doesn't receive gifts meant for your article. You need to claim your website first, by showing you control it. Gifts given before your claim stay where they went.
2. **The community share** of gifts and tips in your own communities.
3. **Paid tiers** that readers subscribe to. On Enterprise you can link your own subscription tiers, so people who already subscribe on your website get the matching perks on Tuvima.
4. **Ember** from Trove members who support your publication.
5. **Staff earnings** routed to the organisation, as above.

Discussion of your articles in other communities does not earn for you, but gifts on the article itself always do.

## What money never does

No plan buys a higher place in the Tuvima front page, search or feeds. Paying for a plan gives you tools, not reach.
