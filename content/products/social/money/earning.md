Anyone on Tuvima can earn from what they share. There is no follower minimum to start.

## Ways to earn

- **Gifts.** People give gifts, paid with [Gold Castar](castar.md#gold-castar), on your posts or your persona page.
- **Tips.** People send you money directly. The smallest tip is $5. You need to confirm who you are before you can receive tips.
- **Ember.** Trove members can support you with their monthly [Ember](castar.md#ember).
- **Subscriptions.** If you run a community, members can subscribe to its paid tiers. See [Earning for your community](community-earnings.md).

## What you keep

| | You | The community | Tuvima |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Gift** | 65% | 5% | 30% |
| **Tip** | 85% | 5% | 10% |

The community's 5% goes to the community the post belongs to, even when someone gives the gift from the Tuvima front page or a post embedded on another website. Gifts on your persona page, outside any community, give that 5% to Tuvima.

A gift always pays you the same, however the giver bought their Gold: on sale, in the app or on the web.

*Example:* someone gives your post a Phoenix, worth $10. You earn $6.50 and the community earns $0.50.

## Creator levels

The more people support you, the bigger your share of gifts. Supporters are the different people who gave you a gift, a tip or Ember, or subscribed, in the last 90 days. Followers and views don't count, because they are easy to fake.

| Level | Supporters in 90 days | Your share of gifts | Extras |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Everyone | any | 65% | Earnings page |
| **Kindler** | 100 | 67% | Thank-you messages for supporters, simple insights |
| **Torchbearer** | 1,000 | 70% | Your own signature gift, full insights, a creator mark |
| **Lightkeeper** | 10,000 | 72% | A named contact at Tuvima, early access to new features |

If your supporters drop, you keep your level for 90 more days. Levels and marks never change how far your posts reach.

## Getting paid

1. Before you can take out money, you confirm who you are in Tuvima ID. Your legal name and bank details stay in Tuvima ID and are never shown in communities.
2. Your first payout becomes available 30 days after that check.
3. After that, earnings are paid to your bank automatically once a month. You can also ask for a payout sooner, for a small fee.

Gift rewards that arrive before your check are kept for 30 days each, then fade if you haven't confirmed who you are. At first, payouts are open to people in the United States.

## If you work for an organisation

Your employer may have its own rules for gifts and tips on your work persona. You can always see what you receive in your settings. See [Organisations and publishers](organisations-and-publishers.md).
