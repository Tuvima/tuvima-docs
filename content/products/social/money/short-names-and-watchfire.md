Every community has a link that never breaks. Members can help a community unlock extras together by lighting its **Watchfire**.

## Short names

Every community already has a [permanent address](../owners/community-address.md) that never breaks, such as `tuvima.com/c/k7f3q2/dragon-masters`.

An established community can also claim a short address, such as `tuvima.com/c/DragonMasters`.

| Who can claim | How many |
| --- | --- |
| An owner on [Tuvima Trove](tuvima-trove.md) Bronze | 1 short name |
| Trove Silver | 2 |
| Trove Gold | 3 |
| Trove Platinum | 5 |
| A community at Watchfire 3 | 1, while it stays at that level |

The Trove numbers count every short name you hold, across all your personas together. The community needs at least 10 members and must be 14 days old.

**If the plan or level ends,** the short name keeps working for 30 days, then it is held for the community for 90 more days. After that another community can claim it. The permanent address never changes. Once a short name goes to another community, the short link opens that community instead, so share the permanent address when a link needs to last.

**Organisations.** Trove Business keeps an organisation's short names for a full first year, even if it pays for only one month. This introductory year happens once per organisation and once per name. Trove Team keeps short names while subscribed.

**Brand names.** A verified organisation can ask for its own brand name back from whoever holds it. Tuvima staff check the claim first, and the current holder gets 30 days' notice. Their permanent address keeps working.

## Watchfire

Watchfire lets members help a community unlock extras together.

- **Lighting it.** Each [Ember](castar.md#ember) you give a community also lights its watchfire. Trove Bronze members get one **spark** a month, which lights a watchfire without sending money.
- **For one month.** A light lasts until your next Trove renewal. Next month you choose again.
- **People, not money.** Levels count how many different people lit it in the last 30 days. One person counts once, however much they spend.

| Level | People lighting it | Extras |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Watchfire 1 | 3 | A community tag members can show beside their name, more custom reactions, an animated community picture |
| Watchfire 2 | 10 | Bigger photo uploads for everyone, an animated banner, extra colours and header layouts |
| Watchfire 3 | 25 | A short address, a custom welcome screen, the biggest uploads |

If fewer people light it, the community keeps its level for 14 days. Extras made meanwhile are paused, not deleted. You can't light your own community or an 18+ community.

## Other ways to support

You can also support a community by [subscribing](castar.md#ember) to it. Anything else a community offers its members, free or paid, is up to that community.

## How support affects what people see

Popular, useful discussions rise on Tuvima. A community gets a small, capped lift when many of its members support it with Ember or Watchfire, compared with its size. A small community with keen supporters can rise above a large community with passive members. Spending more money adds nothing by itself, and "Why you see this" shows when the lift applies.

Paid promotion, when it arrives, appears only in clearly labelled Promoted spaces. Watchfire never buys promotion.
