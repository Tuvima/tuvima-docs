Moderators have a few tools to keep conversations tidy. Every use is added to the [moderation record](run-your-community.md#reports-and-the-moderation-record).

## Pin a conversation

A pinned conversation stays at the top of the list.

- Pin for the **whole community** or for **one forum**.
- Add an optional end date. The pin ends by itself on that day.
- Use **Move up** and **Move down** to set the order of pins.
- Each place can have up to 5 pins.

## The Featured row

The **Featured** row sits on the community home. It shows up to 4 conversations you choose.

## Lock a conversation

A locked conversation cannot get new replies. Add a reason, which members see. Moderators, admins and the owner can still reply.

## Slow mode

Slow mode limits how often each person can post in a conversation.

- Choose a wait from 30 seconds to 1 hour per person.
- Add an optional end, so slow mode switches off by itself.
- Staff are not slowed.

## Move, merge and split

Each of these needs a reason.

- **Move** a conversation to another forum.
- **Merge** a conversation into another one. The old link opens the conversation it joined.
- **Split** replies out into a new conversation.

Merge and split only work between forums that the same people can read. If you move a conversation into a more private forum, it becomes more private too.

## Posts from the moderators

When you reply in a conversation you moderate, tick **Mark as from the moderators** to mark your reply as guidance from the moderators, such as "Please keep this on topic" or a summary of a decision. The reply shows a **From the moderators** label, and in a [big conversation](../guides/posting.md#read-a-big-conversation) it always appears in Catch up, so people who skip ahead still see it. Only people who moderate that forum can mark a reply this way. (These are different from the private [moderator notes](members.md#moderator-notes) your team keeps about members.)
