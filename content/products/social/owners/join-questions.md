When your community uses **Ask to join**, you can ask up to three questions, for example "How did you hear about us?" or "Do you live in Dallas?". People answer them when they send their request.

Owners and admins set them in **Community settings**, then **Join questions**. With no questions, people can ask to join without answering anything.

## Add a question

1. Write **Your question**, up to 200 characters.
2. Pick an **Answer type**:

   | Answer type | What people give |
   | --- | --- |
   | **Short answer** | One line, up to 200 characters. |
   | **Longer answer** | A few sentences, up to 2,000 characters. |
   | **Pick one** | One choice from your list. Tick **Show the choices as a drop-down list** for long lists. |
   | **Pick any** | Any number of choices from your list. |
   | **Yes or no** | A simple yes or no. |
   | **Number** | A number, like an age or a year. |

   For pick types, add 2 to 10 **Choices**, each up to 80 characters.
3. Tick **People must answer this** to make it required, or leave it optional.
4. Use **Preview what people see** to check, then **Save questions**.

Change the order with the move buttons beside each question.

## Who sees the answers

- Only the people who review requests see the answers: by default moderators, admins and the owner.
- Answers are deleted **90 days after the request is decided**.
- Editing a question later does not change answers already given; each answer keeps the question as it was asked.

Do not ask for things people should not share in a community, such as an email address, a phone number or a legal name.
