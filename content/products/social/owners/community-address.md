Every community has a **permanent address**: a short code that never changes, then words from the community's name.

`tuvima.com/c/k7f3q2/dragon-masters`

## How it works

- **The code is the address.** The six letters and numbers after `/c/` are given when the community is created and never change.
- **The words follow the name.** They are there so people can tell what a link is before opening it. Rename the community and the words change, and old links still open it: `tuvima.com/c/k7f3q2/anything` goes to the same place.
- **Names can repeat.** Several communities can be called "Dragon Masters". Each has its own code, and people tell them apart by their picture, size and topic.

## Find and copy it

Owners and admins open **Manage**, then **Address**. The page shows the permanent address with a **Copy address** button. Members can copy it from their browser's address bar.

## Short names

A short name, such as `tuvima.com/c/DragonMasters`, will come with [Tuvima Trove](../money/tuvima-trove.md). **Manage**, **Address** already shows what a community needs for one: at least 10 members, 14 days since it started, and an owner with Trove. Trove is not on sale yet, so no community can claim a short name for now. [Short names](../money/short-names-and-watchfire.md#short-names) explains how they will work, including what happens when Trove ends.

Share the permanent address when a link needs to last: a short name can, in time, go to another community.
