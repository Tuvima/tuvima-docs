Every community is **public** or **private**. Only the owner can change this.

- **Public**: anyone can read, including people who are not signed in. Signed-in people can join.
- **Private**: only members can read anything. If it is listed, everyone else sees just its name and the details you choose to show. If it is unlisted, they see nothing.

You choose this in the **Public or private** step when you [start a community](start-a-community.md), and can change it later.

:::tip
Want most of your community open, with one or two members-only forums? Keep it public and [choose who can see each area](area-access.md) instead.
:::

## Make it private

1. Open **Manage**, then **Joining and privacy**, and find **Who can read this community**.
2. Choose **Private**, then **Save**.
3. Read the summary of what changes and choose **Make private**.

When you save:

- Only members can read posts, forums and the member list.
- Current members keep full access. Nobody is removed and nothing is deleted.
- New people join by asking, or by invite if the community is unlisted. You can change this under [How people join](how-people-join.md).
- Links to your conversations on the Tuvima front page show only **This community is private**. The front-page conversations about them stay, but they no longer lead into your community.
- Search engines are asked to drop your pages. Copies they already saved can take a few days to go.

You can make it public again at any time.

## Listed or unlisted

**Who can find this community** is a separate setting, also for the owner.

| | Listed | Unlisted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public** | In the directory and search. Anyone can read. | Not in the directory. Anyone with the link can read. |
| **Private** | In the directory, showing its name and the details you allow. Only members can read. | Invisible to anyone without an invite. Only members can read. |

## Choose what outsiders see

For a listed private community, its name always shows. Under **How people join**, then **What people outside can see**, owners and admins can also tick:

- **Description**
- **Banner**
- **Rules**
- **Number of members**

All are off at first. The same details show wherever a front-page conversation links to your community.

## What privacy means here

Private keeps your community's posts away from people who are not members. It is not secret messaging: posts are not end-to-end encrypted, so the Tuvima team can read what is stored, as on the rest of Tuvima. Members can also copy or screenshot what they read. See [your privacy on Tuvima](../guides/privacy.md).
