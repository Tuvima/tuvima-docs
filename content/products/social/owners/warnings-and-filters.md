These tools help your moderators act early and fairly. Nothing here bans anyone automatically: moderators always decide.

Owners and admins set them up in **Manage**, then **Warnings and word filters**.

## Newcomers

Newcomers are members who haven't yet met your [newcomer limits](../guides/newcomers.md).

- **Hold first posts from newcomers for review**: their first posts wait in the approval queue until a moderator approves them.
- **What newcomers can use**: switch photos, polls, looking-for-group posts and links on or off for newcomers. Off means it opens up once they are no longer a newcomer.

## Warnings

A moderator can give a member a warning with points.

- **Minor** and **Serious** warnings carry the points you choose (1 and 3 to begin with). A moderator can also choose a number of points, from 1 to 20.
- A warning can name one of your community's rules and carry a message to the member.
- Points expire on their own, after 90 days to begin with.
- Giving or taking back a warning needs the **Suspend and ban** permission, even when the moderator is also removing a post.
- The member sees the warning, the rule and the message, but not which moderator gave it. They must choose **I understand** before posting or replying again, and can still read in the meantime.
- A warning can be taken back from the member's side panel or page. Its points stop counting at once.

### The warning ladder

- **Hold new posts for review**: when a member's points reach a number you choose, their new posts wait in the approval queue for a number of days.
- **Suggest a suspension**: when points reach a higher number, moderators see a suggested suspension. It only happens if a moderator does it.

Set a step to 0 to switch it off.

## Word filters

Add words or phrases your community doesn't allow, up to 200. For each, choose what happens:

- **Hold for approval**: the post waits in the approval queue, and the writer is told it is waiting.
- **Block with a message**: the post isn't posted. The writer sees your message, or "This post has words this community doesn't allow", and can change their words.

## The approval queue

Open **Manage**, then **Approval queue**. Held posts and requests to join wait here, oldest first, and you deal with them one at a time.

- Each item says why it was held, for example a newcomer's first post or a word filter.
- For a post: **Approve**, **Reject** (with an optional rule and a note to the author) or **Skip**.
- For a request to join: **Approve**, **Decline** or **Skip**. Declining doesn't tell the person why.
- If another moderator decides an item first, the queue moves on.

On a phone the buttons stay at the bottom of the screen.

## Removed posts

Open **Manage**, then **Removed posts**. Removed posts can be restored for 30 days. A restored post goes back exactly where it was. If the removal came with a warning, you can also choose to take back its points.
