Leaving a community is a soft step. A member who leaves keeps their persona, posts, level and achievements, and comes back as the same persona. Their roles end when they leave. See [leaving and coming back as a member](../guides/join-a-community.md#leave).

You decide how people come back. Open **Manage**, then **Leaving and coming back** under **Members and access**. Only the owner can change these settings.

## Coming back after leaving

- **Any time** (the default): they rejoin straight away as the persona they had. This works even if your community asks people to be approved or invited, and even during a slow-down on new members.
- **A moderator approves**: their return appears in the [approval queue](warnings-and-filters.md#the-approval-queue) under **Coming back**, and a moderator approves or declines it. An invite still lets a former member straight back in.

## Coming back after a moderator removed someone

This applies to people a moderator [removes from the community](members.md#remove-someone-from-the-community). Choose **A moderator approves** (the default) or **Any time**.

## When someone deletes their persona

Members can delete their persona in your community (see [deleting your persona](../guides/join-a-community.md#delete-your-persona-in-a-community)). For 30 days they can undo it, and their posts show as **Former member**. After that, you choose what happens to their posts:

- **Stay as "Former member"** (the default): no name, link or level.
- **Stay with their name, marked "left"**.
- **Are removed**: replies to them stay.

You also choose when they can start a new persona here: **After 30 days** (the default) or **After 90 days**. Tuvima ID keeps this wait privately, so your community never learns that a new member was someone's old persona.

## What these settings don't do

There is no "can't come back" setting. To keep someone out, [ban them](members.md). Bans and suspensions always stop someone coming back, whatever you choose here.

People always come back as the persona they had. They cannot pick a new name by leaving and joining again; they ask for a rename instead.
