Each category and forum, together called **areas**, has its own rule for who can see it and who can post in it. Use this for a members-only lounge in a public community, a forum for your most trusted members, or an announcements forum where only staff post.

Anyone with the **Manage categories and forums** permission sets this: open **Manage**, then **Forums and categories**, and choose **Who can see and post** next to a category or forum.

## Who can see it

| Choice | Who can open it |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Anyone, without signing in. Only in a public community. |
| **Members only** | Anyone who has joined. |
| **Only some members** | People who match at least one **way in** you add: a level and up (such as **Level 3 (Pathfinder) and up**), a [role](roles.md), a ready-made role and everyone above it (such as **Moderators and up**), or a [member tier](member-tiers.md) (such as **Member tier: Supporter**). |

In a private community everything is already members-only, so you choose between **Members only** and **Only some members**.

The owner and admins always see and post everywhere.

## Forums follow their category

A forum gets its category's rule (**Same as its category**). You can narrow a forum further, but never open it wider than its category. For example, if a category is for Moderators and up, a forum inside it cannot be open to every member.

## Hidden, or shown with a lock

An area someone cannot open is **hidden** from them completely: they do not see it in lists, search, notifications or the Home feed.

Tick **Show it with a lock (name only)** if you would rather people know it exists. They then see its name with a lock and "Only some members can open this", and nothing else. You can write your own **no-access message** instead, up to 280 characters, such as "Become a Supporter to join this forum."

## Who can post

- **Everyone who can see it**, the usual choice.
- **Only some members**, using ways in as above. Others can read but not post, for example in an announcements forum.

## Who can start threads

Starting a new conversation has its own rule, on top of posting:

- **Everyone who can post**, the usual choice.
- **Only some members**, using ways in as above. Everyone else who can post can still reply. For example, in a "Swap Meet" forum, members from Level 3 can start a listing, and every member can reply to one.

## See every forum at once

**Manage**, then **Roles and permissions**, then **Access by forum** shows a grid: one row for each category and each forum under it, and one column for visitors and for each role. Every box shows four small squares, in order: **See it exists**, **Read**, **Start threads** and **Reply**. A filled square means yes, an empty one no, and a lock with a level, such as "L3", means from that level up. Forums that only follow their category are drawn lighter, and forums with rules of their own say **stricter**.

Choose a box to change it for that role in that area. Each choice is **Yes** or **No**; the **Member** column can also choose a level. Only the box you chose changes. If a change can't be made, the panel says why in plain words, for example "A forum can only be stricter than its category". **Show it with a lock** is one setting for the whole area: it applies to everyone who can't read there. Use **Columns** to choose which roles to show, and **Show** to look at one category. The owner and admins can always open everything, so their columns can't be changed.

Every change is kept in the moderation record. Changes about an area are only shown to people who can open it.

**Check a person** answers "why can't this member see that forum?". Search for a member and pick them to see, for every forum, grouped by category, whether they can see it, read, start threads and reply, with one sentence on what stops them, such as "Starting threads needs Level 3. Sam is Level 2."

## Example

In a public gaming community, a "Campaign planning" forum set to **Only some members**, with the way in **Dungeon Master** (a role), and the lock off. Members without the role never see the forum; Dungeon Masters see it beside the others.
