Each category and forum, together called **areas**, has its own rule for who can see it and who can post in it. Use this for a members-only lounge in a public community, a forum for your most trusted members, or an announcements forum where only staff post.

Anyone with the **Manage categories and forums** permission sets this: open **Community settings**, then **Manage categories and forums**, and choose **Who can see and post** next to a category or forum.

## Who can see it

| Choice | Who can open it |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Anyone, without signing in. Only in a public community. |
| **Members only** | Anyone who has joined. |
| **Only some members** | People who match at least one **way in** you add: a level and up (such as **Level 3 (Regular) and up**), a [role](roles.md), a ready-made role and everyone above it (such as **Moderators and up**), or a [member tier](member-tiers.md) (such as **Member tier: Supporter**). |

In a private community everything is already members-only, so you choose between **Members only** and **Only some members**.

The owner and admins always see and post everywhere.

## Forums follow their category

A forum gets its category's rule (**Same as its category**). You can narrow a forum further, but never open it wider than its category. For example, if a category is for Moderators and up, a forum inside it cannot be open to every member.

## Hidden, or shown with a lock

An area someone cannot open is **hidden** from them completely: they do not see it in lists, search, notifications or the Home feed.

Tick **Show it with a lock (name only)** if you would rather people know it exists. They then see its name with a lock and "Only some members can open this", and nothing else.

## Who can post

- **Everyone who can see it**, the usual choice.
- **Only some members**, using ways in as above. Others can read but not post, for example in an announcements forum.

## Example

In a public gaming community, a "Campaign planning" forum set to **Only some members**, with the way in **Dungeon Master** (a role), and the lock off. Members without the role never see the forum; Dungeon Masters see it beside the others.
