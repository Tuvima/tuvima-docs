Members write to your moderator team, and your team writes to members, **as the community**. The member sees your community's name and a **Community** label, never which moderator wrote. Your team sees who wrote each message, so you can work together without stepping on each other.

Only people with the **Handle moderator messages** permission are on the team for this. The ready-made Owner, Admin and Moderator roles have it to begin with; you can change that on the [Roles](roles.md) screen.

## Where conversations wait

Open **Manage > Safety > Approval queue** and choose the **Messages** tab. Its number counts open conversations, and it is part of the queue's total and the **Needs you** card on your community's overview. Filter by:

- **Open**: the member wrote last and is waiting for the team.
- **Waiting for member**: the team wrote last.
- **Closed**: finished conversations.
- **Mine**: conversations you have taken.

Each row shows the member, what it is about, the last line and who has taken it.

## Answer a conversation

1. Open the conversation. The side panel shows how long the member has been here, their recent moderation history, and this conversation's details.
2. Choose **I'll take this** so the rest of the team knows you are on it. Choose **Release** to hand it back.
3. Write your reply, or choose **Send and close** if that settles it. Every team member's message shows **Sent as** your community, **written by** them, on the team side only.
4. **Close** a conversation when it is done. The member can write again to reopen it.

## Team notes

Choose **Team note** above the message box to leave a note for the rest of the team, such as "Asked them to edit the title, check again Friday". Notes show in yellow between the messages. **The member never sees them.**

## Message a member as the community

Choose **Message** on a member's details or on an item in the approval queue. The message says what it is about, for example a post you removed, and reaches the member whatever their message settings. Tick **Take this conversation myself** if you want it under your name. If the member already has an open conversation with your team, your message joins it.

Each message is noted in the [moderation record](run-your-community.md#reports-and-the-moderation-record) as "Moderators messaged this member". The record never holds what was said.

## Pause a member

If someone floods the team, choose **Pause new conversations from this member for 30 days** in the side panel. They can still reply in a conversation that is already open. Choose **Let this member start conversations again** to lift it early. Members can also start only 3 conversations a day in each community.

## What stays private

- Members who leave or are removed can still reply for 30 days, then their conversation closes.
- Your team sees the member's persona only, never their Tuvima ID or email address.
- If a member reports a threat to Tuvima staff, the report shows your community's name, not which moderator wrote.
- Ordinary private messages between members are never shown to moderators. See [private messages](../guides/messages.md#how-private-is-a-private-message).
