**Working today:** making tiers, giving and ending them by hand, tier invite codes, tier-only categories and forums, showing a tier beside names, and notices for members.

**Coming later:** paid tiers, which arrive with payments, and the past-supporter achievement, which is saved now and given out once achievements arrive.

A **member tier** is a kind of membership your community offers, such as **Supporter**, **Patron** or **Season pass**. A tier can open forums only its members see, and it can show beside their name on posts.

A tier gives access and perks, **never influence**. Holding a tier never changes someone's [level](../guides/reputation.md#levels), their Reputation, how much their votes count or how far their posts reach.

Tiers are separate from [roles](roles.md). A role is a job in the community, such as Moderator. A tier is a kind of membership. Someone can have both.

## Who can manage tiers

The owner and admins can make, change and give tiers. The owner can also give the **Manage member tiers** permission to any other role.

Open **Manage**, then **Member tiers**. A community can have up to 50 current tiers; retired tiers don't count.

## Make a tier

Choose **+ New tier** and fill in:

- **Tier name** and an optional description of what it gives.
- **Colour** and **Icon**, from a fixed set chosen to be easy to read in light and dark themes.
- **How people get it:** every tier can be given by hand. Switch on **Invite code** to also let people get it with an [invite](invites.md). Paid tiers come later.
- **How long it lasts:**
  - **Until I end it:** it stays until you end it for that member or retire the tier.
  - **Fixed length:** it ends a set number of days after it is given (1 to 3,650 days).
  - **Renews:** monthly or yearly, for paid tiers later. Until then, a tier that renews is given by hand.
- **Sign-up window** (optional): the dates invite codes for the tier start and stop working.
- **What it gives:**
  - **Name colour**, **Tier icon** and **Tier name as their title:** how the tier shows beside a holder's name on posts.
  - **Skip the Newcomer stage:** [newcomer limits](../guides/newcomers.md) don't apply while someone holds the tier. If it ends before they have finished the Newcomer stage themselves, the limits come back. Their level is never lowered.
  - **Includes everything in:** another tier. A Patron tier can include Supporter, so Patrons can open everything Supporters can.
- **On posts:** whether the tier shows beside holders' names by default. Each holder can still choose for themselves.

Use the arrows to put your tiers in the order members see them.

## Open a forum to a tier

In **Forums and categories**, choose **Who can see and post** next to a category or forum, choose **Only some members**, and add **Member tier** as a way in. See [choose who can see and post in each area](area-access.md).

The moment someone's tier ends, the forum closes for them. Its conversations disappear from their notices, saved and watched lists, search and their own posts, and they are no longer told about new replies there.

## Give or end a tier

Choose **Members** next to a tier to see who has it.

- **Give it:** find a member by name, choose **Give**, and optionally pick an end date. A fixed-length tier ends on its own after its length.
- **End it:** choose **End** next to a member and confirm. Their tier history is kept.

Members get a notice when they are given a tier, a reminder about a week before a tier lasting more than a week runs out, and a notice when you end it, it runs out or the tier is retired.

A tier also ends when:

- its end date passes,
- the member leaves the community, or is banned. No notice is sent then, and joining again does not bring the tier back.

## Tier invite codes

When a tier has **Invite code** switched on, anyone who can [make invites](invites.md) and has the **Manage member tiers** permission can choose **Also gives a tier** when making an invite. People who join with it get the tier too.

People who are already members can type the code on the community's **Tiers** page, under **Have a tier code?**. This works for invites that give a tier with no role.

A tier code only works while the tier is taking codes: inside its sign-up window, and while it is not retired.

## Retire a tier

Retiring a tier stops it taking new members. Members who held it **until ended** lose it straight away and are told. Members with an end date keep it until that date. Areas that let the tier in keep their setting, so you can change them when you are ready.

## What members see

Each community has a **Tiers** page for its members. It shows:

- **Tiers you have**, how you got each one and when it ends,
- the tiers the community offers,
- **What others see**: whether a tier shows beside your name, and which one when you hold more than one. Owners can choose a default, but each member decides for themselves. You can always hide it.

The front page has no member tiers.
