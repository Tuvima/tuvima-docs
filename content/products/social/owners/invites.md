An invite lets someone join your community straight away, whatever your [join setting](how-people-join.md), and even when it is private and unlisted. Admins and the owner can make invites, along with any [role](roles.md) given **Make invites**.

## Make an invite

1. Open **Members**, then the **Invites** tab, and find **Make an invite**.
2. Choose the options:
   - **Ends on** (optional): the invite stops working at the end of that day. Leave it empty to keep it until you turn it off.
   - **Number of people who can use it** (optional): enter 1 for one person; leave it empty for no limit (up to 1,000).
   - **People who use it join as**: a member, or a role you are allowed to give.
   - **Also gives a tier** (optional): people who use it also get a [member tier](member-tiers.md). Only tiers that take invite codes are listed, and you need the **Manage member tiers** permission.
3. Choose **Make invite**.

An invite that gives a moderator-level role hands out real powers, so it must have an end date and a use limit: at most **5 uses and 7 days**.

## Share it

Each invite appears under **Working invites** in three forms:

- a **link**, to send in a message;
- a short **code**, such as `ABCDE-23456`, to read out or write down;
- a **QR code**, to print on a poster or show on a screen at a meet-up.

Use the copy buttons beside the link and code. The list shows how many times each invite has been used and when it ends.

## Turn it off

Choose **Turn off** beside an invite. Its link and code stop working at once. People who already joined with it stay members.

## Keeping invites safe

- Invite pages ask people to sign in before they show the community.
- Tuvima limits how many wrong codes can be tried, so codes are very hard to guess.
- Anyone who has the link can use it until it ends, so share it with care, and prefer a use limit or an end date.
