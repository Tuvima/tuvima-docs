The word "Tuvima" means "discoverable". That is the whole idea: good communities should be easy to find, easy to join and easy to keep coming back to.

## Bring focused communities back

Not long ago, the best conversations online happened in small, focused places: a forum for one hobby, a group for one town, a board for people going through the same thing. Many of those places have faded as everyone moved into a few huge meeting spaces, where specific conversations get lost in the noise.

Tuvima is built for the specific. A community here is meant for a group with something real in common, like "board game players in Dallas" rather than "games". Big, broad topics such as news still have a home, on the Tuvima front page, sorted by categories and tags.

## Help communities grow and thrive

A community is only as good as the people who look after it. Tuvima gives owners the tools to welcome newcomers, keep out spam, moderate fairly and see how they are doing, without running their own servers. Every community on Tuvima is also discoverable from the front page and the community directory, so the right people can find it.

## A sensible expectation of privacy

You should be able to have a conversation without handing over your whole identity. On Tuvima you can use one global persona everywhere, or a separate private persona in a community, and your email address is never shown to anyone.

That privacy has limits, and we say so plainly. Tuvima is a place for conversations, not a secret messaging service. Read [your privacy on Tuvima](../guides/privacy.md) to see exactly what is and is not protected.

## Your data should work for you

We keep what we need to run Tuvima, and we want it to benefit you directly: helping you find communities that fit, bringing back conversations you care about and helping the communities you love grow. There are no advertising trackers, and we do not sell your data.
