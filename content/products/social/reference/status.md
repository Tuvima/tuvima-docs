Tuvima is in Early Access: it is being built and tested, and is not open to everyone yet. This page is the honest list of what works and what is still to come. Planned features may change before they arrive.

## Ready today

| Area | What works |
| --- | --- |
| Signing in | Tuvima ID sign-in, your global persona, private personas per community, two-step sign-in. |
| Front page | Trending and New feeds, 16 categories with subtopics, tags, reactions, posting links and discussions, search. |
| Communities | Directory with New and rising, joining and leaving, community search, unlisted communities. |
| Conversations | Formatting, link previews with click-to-play, content warnings, quotes, mentions, edit history, flat and nested views. |
| Coming back | Bell notices, watch, save, the You page and persona editing. Reply and mention emails are being switched on. |
| Reactions and points | Five reactions on every post. Reputation points and levels are already being counted, and a post removed for breaking the rules loses its points. |
| Safety | Reports, hiding and removing posts, suspensions and bans, moderation records, newcomer limits, spam protection. |
| Owner tools | Setup steps, ranks, members page, numbers, launch checklist, themes, banners, page blocks, categories and forums, and labels on Tuvima. |
| Help | A help centre and draft rules and policies. |

## Coming later

| Feature | What it will do |
| --- | --- |
| Seeing your Reputation and level | Your level and Reputation next to your name, your persona page breakdown, badges, Helpful this month, and owners naming their own levels. See [Reputation and levels](../guides/reputation.md). |
| A feed made for you | A front page shaped by the communities and subjects you follow. |
| Connect your website | Bring articles from a website you already run into your community and the front page. |
| Your own web address | Move your community to a web address you own, with old Tuvima links forwarding to it. |
| Handing over ownership | Pass a community to another member. |
| Uploading pictures and video | Today posts link to media hosted elsewhere. |
| Status page | A separate page showing whether Tuvima is working normally. |
| Tuvima Support | A public community where you can ask the Tuvima team for help. |
| Final rules and policies | Our draft terms and policies, after a lawyer's review. |

For Tuvima ID's own plans, see [what's coming to Tuvima ID](/products/id/reference/whats-coming.md).
