Tuvima is built so you can talk openly in a community without handing everyone your whole life. This page explains the privacy you get, and just as plainly, the privacy you do not get.

## The short version

- Your sign-in email stays in Tuvima ID. Communities, owners, moderators and other members never see it.
- Each community sees only the profile you chose to use there. Nobody else can tell that your profiles belong to the same person.
- Tuvima is **not** a secret or encrypted messaging service. The people who run Tuvima can reach the information it holds when they need to. Share accordingly.

## What stays where

![Tuvima ID keeps your email, security settings and the list of which profiles are yours. Tuvima keeps profiles, communities and posts. A short-lived pass tells Tuvima only which profile is acting, and emails go out from Tuvima ID using a separate address for each community.](../assets/what-stays-where.svg)

When you sign in, Tuvima ID hands Tuvima a short-lived pass that says only "this is profile X". It does not say who you are or which other profiles you have. When someone replies to you and you have asked for email, Tuvima asks Tuvima ID to send it, and Tuvima ID does so from that community's own address for you. Your real address never leaves Tuvima ID.

## What each community sees

| Who | What they can see |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone, including visitors | Your profile's handle, display name and picture, and what you post in public places. |
| Members of a community | The same, for the profile you use in that community. |
| A community's moderators and owner | The above, plus reports you make in their community and their own moderation records. |
| Nobody on Tuvima | Your sign-in email, your other profiles, or which account a profile belongs to. |

A private profile is private in one sense: other people cannot connect it to you. What you post with it is still visible to the members of that community.

## The privacy you do not have

We would rather be honest than impressive.

- **Tuvima can see what is on Tuvima.** Posts, including posts in communities that are not open to everyone, are stored in a way the Tuvima team can read. They are not end-to-end encrypted, the kind of protection where even the company cannot read your messages.
- **Tuvima ID knows which profiles are yours.** It has to, to sign you in. The everyday tools our staff use do not show that link, and we look only when there is a real reason, such as stopping abuse, keeping people safe or answering a lawful request. But the link exists, and people who run Tuvima can reach it.
- **Moderation leaves a record.** When a moderator or staff member acts on a post or a profile, that action is logged and kept, so decisions can be checked and challenged.
- **Deleting is not instant erasure yet.** When you delete a post, it disappears from view. Full, permanent deletion is being designed before Tuvima opens to everyone.
- **What others copy is out of our hands.** Anyone who can read a post can quote it, screenshot it or remember it.

## Use good judgment

No feature we build can stop you sharing something you later regret. Before you post, ask yourself whether you would be comfortable if it were read by someone you did not expect. In particular:

- Do not post passwords, bank details, your home address or anything you would not say out loud in a busy room.
- Remember that a private profile hides who you are only until you say something that gives it away.
- Be careful with links and with people who ask you to move a conversation somewhere else.

## Why your data is here at all

We keep what we need to run your account and your conversations, and we want it to work for you: to help you find communities that fit, to bring back conversations you care about and to help the communities you love grow. Tuvima has no advertising trackers, does not use third-party analytics and does not sell your data. Numbers about how Tuvima is used come from our own records.
