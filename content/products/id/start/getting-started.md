By the end of this page you will have a Tuvima ID and a public handle, and you will be ready to join a community. It takes about three minutes.

## Before you start

You need an email address you can open now. It stays private: nobody on Tuvima will see it.

## Create your Tuvima ID

1. On Tuvima, choose **Sign in** at the top of the page. You move to the Tuvima ID sign-in page.
2. Enter your email address and follow the steps to create your account.
3. If you see **Check your email to finish signing up**, open the message we sent and follow its link, then choose **Sign in again**.

Until your email is confirmed you can look around, but you cannot create a profile or take part in communities.

:::note
If you see "Lots of people are joining right now", new sign-ups are paused for a short while when lots of people are joining at once. Try again in about an hour. If you already have a Tuvima ID, you can still sign in.
:::

## Choose your handle

After signing in you return to Tuvima, which asks you to **Choose your handle**. Your handle is the public name shown next to your posts, as `handle @ tuvimaid.com`.

- Use 3 to 30 letters (A to Z), numbers and underscores. Capitals do not make a different handle.
- Some names are reserved, for example names that look like Tuvima staff, and look-alike letters from other alphabets are refused.
- You can change your display name and picture at any time. Choose the handle with care: it can change only once every 30 days.

## Check the result

You see **You're all set**. Your handle now shows in the menu at the top of Tuvima, and your [account page](../guides/your-account.md) on Tuvima ID lists your Tuvima profile as created.
