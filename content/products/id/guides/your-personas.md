A persona is the name people see when you post. You have one **global persona**, used in any community you join with it, and you can have a **private persona** for a single community. Your personas page on Tuvima ID lists them all in one place. Only you can see it, and communities never learn which personas belong to you.

## Open the page

Sign in to Tuvima ID and choose **Personas** in the menu. On a phone, the menu is the row of tabs under the top bar.

## Find a persona

- **Search** for a community by name. Your global persona shows up when it is used in a matching community.
- **All, Global, Private** narrow the list to one kind of persona.
- Each row shows the kind of persona, how many communities it is used in and when it was made. A long list shows 50 at a time, with **Show more** at the bottom.

## What you see for each persona

Choose a persona to open its details.

- **Name and picture**: your global persona's name, picture and web address are set on Tuvima. You can change the name or web address once every 30 days, and your old name is held for 90 days so nobody else can take it straight away. A private persona is renamed by asking that community's admins, who approve or decline the new name.
- **Relay email addresses**: each community gets its own email address for you, so no community sees your sign-in email. An address is made the first time a community emails you. You can see whether email from that community is allowed or stopped, and change it in [email and alerts](email.md).
- **Where it is used**: each community, with the day you joined or left. Follows, topics and community settings live on Tuvima, so you manage them there.

Names and pictures are kept on Tuvima rather than Tuvima ID, so this page names each persona by its kind and community.

## Privacy

This page is built only from what Tuvima ID already holds. Opening it sends nothing to Tuvima, and links from it to Tuvima do not say which persona you were looking at.
