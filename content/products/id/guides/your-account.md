Your account page on Tuvima ID is the one place that shows everything tied to your account. Only you can see it.

## Open your account

Go to Tuvima ID and choose **See your account**, or sign in first. The page has two parts.

A menu on every account page takes you to **Overview** (this page), **Personas**, **Sign-in and security**, **Email and alerts** and **Your feed**. On a phone, the menu is the row of tabs under the top bar.

Tuvima ID opens in dark mode. Use **Dark**, **Light** or **Device** in the top bar to change it; **Device** follows your phone or computer's setting. Your choice is remembered in that browser.

On a phone, tablet or touch screen, every button, link, switch and tick box is at least 44 pixels to tap, about the size of a fingertip, and the words beside a switch or tick box work as part of it. Text is 16 pixels on a phone, so your phone doesn't zoom in when you tap a box to type.

## Account-wide

These settings apply to your whole Tuvima ID, not to any one community.

- **Sign-in email**: the address you sign in with. Communities never see it.
- **Account created**: the day you joined.
- **global persona**: whether your global persona has been created yet.
- **Your personas**: opens [your personas](your-personas.md), every persona you use and where.
- **Email about replies and mentions**: opens [your email choices](email.md).
- **Two-step sign-in**: whether it is on, with a link to [turn it on or manage it](two-step-sign-in.md).

## Your communities

Every community you have joined is listed with:

- **Persona used**: your global persona, or a private persona for that community.
- **Status**: member, the day you left, or **Being deleted** with the day it becomes final if you deleted your persona there. Once it is final the row says **Deleted on** that day and shows only the community's name, with **A new persona here from** the day you can join again with a new persona. Only you see that date.
- **Joined**: the day you joined.
- **Your posts in this community**: a link to a page on Tuvima where you can see, and delete, what you posted there. It works after you leave, too.

Each community's details are kept and shown separately, even if you used the same persona in several.

## Sign out

**Sign out** ends your session on Tuvima ID and on Tuvima together, so nobody using the same device afterwards is still signed in.

## Contact the Tuvima team

If something is wrong with your account, you have found a security problem, or you have a question about your privacy or a community that was held or removed, use the **contact form** on Tuvima ID. Only Tuvima staff read it. Community owners and moderators never see it. We reply to your sign-in email, usually within two working days. To prevent spam, you can send a few messages a day.
