Tuvima ID delivers your email and phone alerts for every Tuvima app. Each community or app decides what is worth telling you about, and only inside it; Tuvima ID decides when and how it reaches you, the same way for all of them.

:::note
Email and phone alerts are being switched on during Early Access. You can set your choices now, and they apply once sending starts.
:::

Open [your account page](your-account.md) and choose **Email and alerts**.

## How email works

- Email starts off. You switch it on inside a community, one conversation at a time. See [email about a conversation](/products/social/guides/keep-up.md#email-about-a-conversation).
- Everything waiting is gathered into **one email, at most one every 8 hours**, across every app that uses Tuvima ID. Nothing is sent during your quiet hours.
- Each email names who and where, never what anyone wrote, and links back. The footer says why you got it, when the next email can come at the earliest, and how to stop.
- Tuvima ID sends it, so communities never learn your address.

Security and sign-in email from Tuvima ID always arrive straight away.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours are on by default, from 10pm to 7am in your time zone. During them no email or phone alert arrives from any app. Alerts still collect in each app's bell, email waits, and when quiet hours end each device gets one alert saying how many arrived.

Change the times or your time zone under **Quiet hours**. Emails show their times in your time zone too.

## Phone alerts

- Switch phone alerts on or off for a device in the app's notification settings on that device.
- **Your devices** lists every device that gets alerts. Each one gets alerts for all your personas. Choose **Remove** for one you no longer use.
- **Show the post's text in alerts** is off by default. Off, alerts say who and where, like "kai replied in Best low tide". On, they also show the first part of what was written, where the app allows it. Anyone who can see your lock screen can read it.

## Choose who may email you

Under **Allowed to email you**, every community you are in has its own switch. Switch one off and it can no longer email you, whatever you chose inside it. **Alert settings** takes you to that community's own page, where you choose what it tells you about.

## A separate address for each community

Each community has its own address for you, shown on this page and at the bottom of every email about that community. It tells you at a glance where a message came from. Your real address stays private.

Choose **Swap address** to replace one with a new one. The old address stops for good. When you leave a community, its address stops too.

## Pause all email

**Pause all email** stops every community and app from emailing you until you turn it back on. Security and billing email from Tuvima ID still arrive. Every email also has a link to stop email from that community.
