Every payment on Tuvima goes through Tuvima ID: community subscriptions, tips, digital items, add-ons and apps. Your card stays with our payment provider. Tuvima and the people you buy from never see it.

Payments are being built now and you can't buy anything yet. This page describes how they will work.

## Approving a purchase

When you choose to buy something, a Tuvima ID page opens. It shows:

- what you're buying and the price, including how often it renews
- who it's sold for: every receipt reads **Sold by Tuvima on behalf of** the community, creator or developer
- the card you'll pay with

Nothing costs less than $2, so card fees never outweigh what the seller and Tuvima receive.

Nothing is charged until you choose **Pay**. Approving one purchase never allows another, and no community or app gets a spending allowance.

## Subscriptions and renewals

- A subscription renews on its date at the price you approved.
- **Stop renewing** at any time from **Purchases**. You keep everything you've paid for until the end of the period, and nothing more is charged.
- **A higher price always needs your yes.** You'll see the new price on **Purchases** at least a week before the renewal it applies to. If you don't approve it, the subscription simply stops at the end of what you've paid for. A lower price applies at your next renewal without you doing anything.
- **If your card is declined**, the subscription's benefits end on the date you'd paid up to. We'll try the card again over the next week. If it works, a fresh period starts on the day it's paid, so you never pay for days you didn't have.

## Refunds

If you're not happy with a subscription, you can get back the part you haven't used. If the plan included Gold you've already spent, its value comes off the refund, which never goes below zero. A refunded subscription ends on the day of the refund.

## What sellers and apps can see

A community, creator or app you buy from only learns:

| They see | They never see |
|---|---|
| The persona you bought with | Your sign-in email |
| The amount and what it was for | Your card or bank details |
| Whether it's paid, and until when | Your other personas |
| A reference number for that one purchase | Anything else you've bought |

Each purchase gets its own reference number, so references can't be used to connect your purchases together.

## Where the money goes

The seller gets most of every sale. Tuvima's share pays for hosting, card fees and support, and card fees always come out of Tuvima's share, never the seller's.

| What you buy | Seller or receiver | Community | Tuvima |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community subscription, digital item, add-on or app feature | 80% | | 20% |
| Cash tip (at least $5) | 85% | 5% | 10% |
| Gift | 65% to 72%, rising as creators grow | 5% to 8% | 30% down to 20% |

When there's no community involved, for example a tip on someone's persona page, the community's share stays with Tuvima.

## Purchases

**Purchases** in Tuvima ID lists every subscription and receipt from every app in one place. Only you can see it. From there you can stop or restart renewing, and approve or decline a new price.

## 18+ communities

Communities marked 18+ can't sell anything yet. Paying in them will work through a separate adult currency, added later.
