Tuvima products are built so that your media and your data stay with you. These principles apply to every product.

## What we promise

- **Your data stays on your hardware.** Tuvima apps run on your own computer or server. There is no Tuvima-hosted account service and no built-in telemetry.
- **Nothing is shared by default.** Outside requests, such as metadata lookups or model downloads, happen only when you turn on the feature that needs them, and they are listed in the product's documentation.
- **No tracking on this site.** This documentation site has no analytics, no third-party scripts and no cookies for tracking. Fonts and search are served from the site itself.
- **Your files are protected.** Original media files are never changed unless you ask for it.
